# Dashboards and Saved Lists

The workspace's own dashboards, the lists people save as reports, and
reports sent by mail.

## Saved Reports

A list's **Report** view, saved under a name with **Save As**, goes in the
rail of the app whose list it was. Anybody may save one. An administrator's
is shown to everybody; anybody else's is in their own rail. Deleted, it
leaves the rail.

## Dashboards

Under the rail's **Dashboards**, an administrator makes **Dashboards** of
**Charts** and **Number Cards**. Everybody sees them under {one} ›
**Dashboards**, and each chart shows only to those who may read what it
counts. The dashboards that come with the apps (Accounts, Stock, CRM) are on
their own app's rail, a company on every chart.

## Reports by Mail

Automation › **Reports by Mail**, or **Setup Auto Email** on a report's menu,
emails a report daily, weekly or monthly to the addresses you enter. The
report shows what the person who set it up can see. Only administrators
create them.

## What Isn't Offered

- A report of any kind but one saved from a list's **Report** view, since
  the others can read past what the reader may open.
- A chart or a number card of type **Custom**, which runs code an app wrote.
- A report by mail that runs as somebody else.

## With {one_ai}

{one_ai} suggests a saved report, a dashboard or a report by mail as a card, with
where it is shown and to whom. Nothing is made until you approve it.
