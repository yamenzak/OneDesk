# Custom Collections

A custom collection is something your workspace keeps that no app came with:
memberships, vehicles, rooms, contracts, inspections.

## Adding One

1. Click **Add Collection**.
2. Tell {one_ai} what you keep, such as "the vans we keep: registration, make,
   mileage, the date we bought it and whether it is in use."
3. {one_ai} designs its fields and shows a card with its name, app and fields.
4. **Approve** creates it.

## Where It Is

In the rail of the app it belongs to, in the group you choose: one the rail
already has, such as **Sales** in {one_crm}, or a new one. Without a choice it
goes under **Your Records**. People who use that app create and edit its
records, and its managers delete them. A collection in **One** is for
everyone. Opening a collection opens its records.

## Its Fields and Numbers

**Field types**: text, numbers, money, percentages, dates and times, checks,
selects, phone numbers, ratings, durations, files and images, and links to
records you can open or to another collection. The first text field is each
record's title.

**Numbering**: each record gets a number from the collection's initials, such
as CV-00001 for a Company Van. Change it in Setup › **Numbering**. See
[Numbering](numbering).

## Moving, Changing and Deleting

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Move one | Tick it and click **Actions › Move**, then choose the app and the group. Its records stay as they are, and the people who use them follow the app. |
| Change one | Tick it and click **Actions › Change**, or ask {one_ai}, such as "Add a colour to Company Van." A removed field is hidden, not deleted, so no data is lost. |
| Delete one | Delete its records first: a collection that still has records can't be deleted. A deleted collection is in {one} › **Recycle Bin**, and restoring it brings back its records list. |
