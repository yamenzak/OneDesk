# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens {one_studio}'s settings. Only the
workspace's administrators open them.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Numbering** | How each kind of record is numbered. See [Numbering](numbering). |
| **Printing** | The letter heads and print formats documents are printed and sent on. See [Printing](printing). |
| **Approvals** | Who approves which records, step by step. See [Approvals](approvals). |
| **Lists** | **Print Headings**, the titles a printed record can carry in place of its own. |
| **Notifications** | {notification_center}, on {one_studio}'s part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Your Data** | {trust_center}, on {one_studio}'s part of the agreements. |

{one_studio} has no roles of its own: whoever administers the workspace uses all
of it, and nobody else sees it.
