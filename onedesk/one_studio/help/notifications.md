# Being Told

What {one_studio} tells the workspace's administrators, and when. Every one
comes in {one}; **Notifications** in Settings changes how each person hears of
each one. No one is told of their own change.

| Notification | When | By email unless you turn it off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Extensions Failing** | Each morning, the extensions that ran into errors the day before. Not sent when none did. | Yes |
| **Extension Turned On** | When another administrator turns one on, with what it does and where it runs. | Yes |
| **Extension Turned Off**, **Extension Deleted** | When another administrator turns one off or deletes it. | No |
| **Extension Fixed** | When Fix With {one_ai} has mended an extension, to whoever asked. | Yes |
| **Collection Added** | When another administrator adds a custom collection, with its fields and where its records are. | Yes |
| **Collection Changed**, **Collection Deleted** | When another administrator changes or deletes one. | No |
| **Form Customized** | When another administrator changes a form for everyone, or resets it. | No |

**Approval Waiting**, **Approval Answered**, **Automation Notice** and
**Webhooks Failing** are listed under {one} in NotificationCenter. See
[Approvals](approvals), [Automations](automations) and [Webhooks](webhooks).
