**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_studio} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

OneStudio has a foot like the other apps: **Settings**, a tour of the rail, **Help** and its version, which opens What's New. Help has a page for each thing you do here, from **Extensions** and **Custom Fields** to **Numbering**, **Printing**, **Approvals**, **Automations** and **Webhooks**, and OneAI answers your questions from the same pages. In **Printing**, a standard letter head says that only **Default** and **Disabled** can be changed on it. OneStudio's part of the Terms and the AI Addendum is written in shorter sentences, and says the same as before.

In **Custom Fields**, the forms of OneAI are listed under **OneAI** in the **App** filter.

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.

{one_studio} needs {one_ai}: turning {one_studio} on in **Turn Apps On or Off** turns {one_ai} on too, and {one_ai} stays on while {one_studio} is.
