# Working as a Team

A shared address, such as the workspace's own or sales@, is read by everyone
who holds it. These keep a team from answering twice or not at all.

## Who Has It

**Assign** on a conversation gives it to one of the people who hold the
mailbox. They're told, and it's among their tasks until it's done. Above the
list, **Open**, **Mine**, **Unassigned** and **Done** show the conversations
each way.

## Done

**Mark Done** when nothing more is needed. It leaves **Open** for **Done**
and closes its assignment. If the customer writes again, it opens by itself.

## Notes for the Team

**Add a note for the team** writes beside the conversation, for the people
who hold the mailbox. It's never sent to anybody outside.

## Who Is Replying

While somebody writes a reply, the others see **is writing a reply** on the
conversation, so two people don't answer the same mail.
