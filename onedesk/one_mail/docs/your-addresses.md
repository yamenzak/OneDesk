# Your Addresses

Every workspace and every person in it gets an address the day they join, before
anyone sets anything up.

## Addresses the Workspace Gives

| Address | Looks like | Made when | Who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The workspace's** | acme@m.4dl.app | The workspace is made | Workspace Administrators, and whoever they add |
| **Yours** | name.acme@m.4dl.app | You are added to the workspace | You |

**Your own address** uses the name whoever added you chose, or else your first
name, and it can't be changed once given. You can send from it as well as
receive, unless an administrator turns that off for everyone.

> **The workspace's mailbox can move.** It stays the workspace's mailbox until
> an administrator makes a connected company mailbox the workspace's instead.

## Five Kinds of Mailbox

Every mailbox is one of five kinds, by whose it is.

| Kind | Whose it is | For example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | The company's, where mail to the workspace arrives | acme@m.4dl.app |
| **Shared** | A team's | sales@yourcompany.com |
| **System** | The address the workspace gives each person | ahmad.yourcompany@m.4dl.app |
| **Organization** | A person's, at one of the company's domains | ahmad@yourcompany.com |
| **Private** | A person's anywhere else | Gmail, Outlook |

> **The company's domains** are the workspace's mail domain, the domain of every
> shared mailbox, and every domain added under **Settings › Setup**, so nobody
> types them twice.
