- **Roles and Access**, at the end of OneMail's settings, opens
  AccessCenter on OneMail: what each role may do, everybody's OneMail role,
  changed right there, and everybody given more on one mailbox. It replaces
  the Roles tab.
- **Notifications**, beside it, opens NotificationCenter on OneMail: an
  Email and a Push switch for each thing OneMail tells you, and for
  administrators what it sends in the workspace and on which channels.
- The gear in the bell's list opens NotificationCenter for all of One.
