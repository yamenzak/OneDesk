- Ask OneAI to archive, move, star or mark conversations, make or rename a
  folder, file a conversation on a record, or turn your out of office on or
  off. It answers with a card, and nothing changes until you press
  **Approve**.
- Whoever sets OneMail up can also ask it to turn a rule on or off, share a
  workspace mailbox with someone, add the company's mail domain, or change
  whether people may connect their own mailboxes.
- A card does only what your role lets you do in OneMail yourself. A rule
  OneAI suggests for an address you don't manage now waits for a Mail
  Manager, as one you write does.
