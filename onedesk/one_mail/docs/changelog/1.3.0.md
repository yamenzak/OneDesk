What you write is now kept as you type. Close the email window or the tab and it waits in **Drafts**, and a reply waits on its conversation with **Continue Draft**.

- **A shared mailbox works as a team inbox.** **Assign** gives a conversation to one of the people who hold the mailbox, **Mark Done** puts it away until the customer writes again, and **Open**, **Mine**, **Unassigned** and **Done** sort the list. Notes for the team sit beside the mail, and you see when a colleague is already writing a reply.
- **Who wrote shows beside their mail**: the customer, supplier, lead, employee or applicant, with their open deals, orders, invoices, issues, projects, leave and claims.
- **Create** turns a mail into a lead, an opportunity, an issue, a task, a non conformance or a purchase order, filled in from it and linked to it.
- **Snooze** a conversation until later, and **Remind Me** if nobody replies.
- A mail scheduled to send later shows when it goes, with **Edit Before It Sends**.
- Mail now finds its way to tasks, work orders, work sent to suppliers, payment reminders, leave, interviews, job offers and quality records by their numbers, and to customers, suppliers and applicants by their own address. Each of these has a **Mail** tab.
- On a mailbox you connected, a draft is kept in its own Drafts too, so your phone's mail app shows it.
- **Forward** reads إعادة توجيه in Arabic and Weiterleiten in German.
- Keys: **d** marks a conversation done or opens it again, **b** snoozes it, and **Shift a** assigns it.
- **OneMail reads like Gmail, and looks like every other list in One.** The folder's name is the heading with **New Email** beside it, **Open**, **Mine**, **Unassigned** and **Done** sit under it as a list's states do, and the list fills the window, one conversation to a line. A conversation opens in its place, its subject the heading with **Archive**, **Delete** and the rest beside it. The two buttons at the heading's far end put the list and the conversation side by side again.
- On a tablet, or with a pane beside it, OneMail shows the list or the conversation, as on a phone, instead of squeezing both.
- **A conversation keeps its panel on the right, like a record's**: **Assign**, **Linked To**, **Create**, **Remind Me**, who wrote and the team's notes. Beside the subject stay **Mark Done**, the mail's own actions and **Snooze**. On a phone the panel opens from one line under the subject.
- **Back** in the browser closes the conversation you opened and returns to the list, as in Gmail.
- The email window is drawn like the rest of One, light or dark, with one **Full Screen** button.
- A conversation you have read stays read: every few minutes OneMail was marking each one's newest mail unread again.
- In the dark theme, a message is drawn dark too.
