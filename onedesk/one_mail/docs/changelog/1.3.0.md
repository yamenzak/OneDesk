- **Getting Started** in Settings lists what's left to set up: your
  company's mail domain, whether people may connect their own mailboxes, and
  the mail templates. **Set Up** takes you to the right place, and **Mark as
  Done** takes a step off the list.
- A new workspace is asked for its mail domain and about private mailboxes
  while it is set up, and can leave either for later.
- Only people whose role can do a step see it.
