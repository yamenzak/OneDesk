- Gravatar photos and logos from the web are now optional services in the
  Privacy Policy and the Subprocessors list. Each is off until a workspace
  administrator turns it on, which agrees to it for the workspace. While
  they're off, people show their initials and organizations show no logo.
- **TrustCenter**, the shield at the foot of OneMail, replaces the Legal tab
  in Settings. It shows what OneMail sends out and to whom, every agreement,
  and what is changing in them.
- OneMail's parts of the Privacy Policy and the AI Addendum are rewritten in
  shorter sentences, one point to each.
