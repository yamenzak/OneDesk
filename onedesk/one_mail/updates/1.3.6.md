OneCloud's folder is `one_cloud` and its module One Cloud (P2.1 of docs/run/RUN.md); this module's imports and paths into it follow. Nothing changes for people.

TrustCenter's folder is `trust_center` and its module Trust Center (P2.5 of docs/run/RUN.md); this module's legal.py and its imports into it follow. Nothing changes for people.
Its keys' labels are marked with the registry's _lt, and a face's address hashed with frappe's sha256_hash, rather than copies (P3.6 of docs/run/RUN.md). Nothing changes for people.

Its parts are named for the centres that read them (P2.10 of docs/run/RUN.md): its Help pages are in `help/`, its versions in `updates/`, legal.py is `trust.py`, ai.py is `agent.py`, and abilities.py and access.py are one `access.py`. Nothing changes for people.

When a mailbox uses its credits for the month, the notification says how many credits, such as "500 credits".

Its manifest names what its agent is called, and the registry makes that agent a user with its User roles alone (P8.1 of docs/run/RUN.md). Nothing changes for people. Its receptionist has `TOOLS` incoming and hand_on, a level for each REACH call, and opens the mail of a mailbox OneAI reads (access.py).
