A mail rule that replies with {one_ai} now has {one_mail}'s receptionist write the reply. It counts against the receptionist's credits for the month in {ai_center}, and while agents are turned off there, such a rule sends no reply.

{one_mail}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
