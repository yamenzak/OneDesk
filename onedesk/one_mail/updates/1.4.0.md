**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_mail} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

**Getting Started** in {one_mail}'s Settings opens {setup_center} on {one_mail}'s steps. **Your Company's Mail** and **Private Mailboxes** have their own form there, the same one asked when the workspace was set up, and **Mail Templates** opens its tab.

When a mailbox uses its credits for the month, the notification says how many credits, such as "500 credits".

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.

**Mailboxes** in **Settings** says **No mailboxes** when you hold none, rather than staying blank.

If you administer the workspace, each thing {one_mail}'s agent may do is named in {ai_center}, in your language.

{one_mail} has a page in {one_marketplace}, for administrators: what it does, what it brings, whether your plan includes it and the credits it uses.
