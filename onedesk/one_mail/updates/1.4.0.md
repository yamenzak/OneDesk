**Getting Started** in {one_mail}'s Settings opens {setup_center} on {one_mail}'s steps. **Your Company's Mail** and **Private Mailboxes** have their own form there, the same one asked when the workspace was set up, and **Mail Templates** opens its tab.

When a mailbox uses its credits for the month, the notification says how many credits, such as "500 credits".

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.
