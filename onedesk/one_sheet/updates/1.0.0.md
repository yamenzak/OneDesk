OneWorkbook is the business's spreadsheet, kept in OneCloud beside the rest
of your files. This is the version it is released at.

- **New › Workbook** in OneCloud makes one, blank or from a template your
  business keeps.
- Sheets, formulas, formatting, charts, pivot tables and notes, and
  everybody with a workbook open sees the others' changes as they happen.
- Excel and CSV files open in OneWorkbook and are saved back into themselves,
  and a workbook is an Excel file on a network drive.
- Version History keeps what a workbook was, cell by cell, and goes back to
  it.
- Print under your letter head, and keep the PDF in OneCloud or on the
  record.
- A record's table opens as a sheet, and the record saves itself from it.
- A sheet reads the business and the record it is for.
- OneAI makes workbooks, writes cells and leaves notes as cards you approve.
  This adds a part to the AI Addendum.
- AccessCenter decides who may keep company templates.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneWorkbook Is.
