OneWorkbook is called **OneSheet** from now on. Your workbooks, templates
and versions are where they were and work as before; only the name on the
screen, in Help and in the agreements is new. A record's table opens with
**Open in OneSheet**. Its roles are now **OneSheet User** and **OneSheet
Manager**, held by the same people.

{one_sheet} has its own place on the rail: **Recent Workbooks** lists every workbook you can open, the last changed first, and **New Workbook** makes one in your own files. An administrator can now turn {one_sheet} off. Workbooks already made still open, read-only, and still download as Excel.

{one_sheet} has a page in {one_marketplace}, for administrators: what it does, what it brings, whether your plan includes it and the credits it uses.
