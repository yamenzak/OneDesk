Excel files keep their look when edited here: only the cells you change are
saved into the file, so its fonts, colours, borders, comments, data
validation and conditional formatting stay as they were.

When an Excel file has things OneWorkbook doesn't keep, such as charts or
slicers, a bar names them and your changes stay in the
workbook until you choose **Save Over Original** or **Save as Copy**. Files
with macros (.xlsm) and older .xls files open read-only. Saves within 10
minutes of each other are one version, and while somebody edits a file in
OneOS it opens read-only here and says who. In OneCloud, a file somebody
has open in OneWorkbook says so under its name.
