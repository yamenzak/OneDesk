OneWorkbook is called **OneSheet** from now on. Your workbooks, templates
and versions are where they were and work as before; only the name on the
screen, in Help and in the agreements is new. A record's table opens with
**Open in OneSheet**. Its roles are now **OneSheet User** and **OneSheet
Manager**, held by the same people.
