# Who Can Do What

A workbook is a file in OneCloud, so whoever may open the file may open the
workbook, and whoever may change the file may change it. Sharing it is
sharing the file. A record's workbook follows the record.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Make workbooks, and work on the ones shared with them. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also keep a workbook as a template for everyone. |

## Without the Role

A Workspace Administrator can do everything, and changes or deletes any
template.
