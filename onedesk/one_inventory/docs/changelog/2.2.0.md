With more than one company, every stock, buying and asset record shows its
**Company**, starting as yours, and its list has a **Company** column and
filter. Pickers offer only that company's warehouses, and Home, **Stock
Summary**, **Reorder** and an item's figures stay your company's. A workspace
with one company looks as it did.
