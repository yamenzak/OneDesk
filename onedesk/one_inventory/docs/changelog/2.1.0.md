- On **Stock Summary** and **Reorder**, **/** goes to the search, and on
  **Reorder** **O** drafts the purchase orders. Change them in
  **Shortcuts**.
- An item's last purchase price and supplier show only to whoever may read
  purchase receipts or purchase orders.
- **Reorder** lists only the material requests you may open.
- **Fix** in **Inventory Check** changes the company's warehouses and
  accounts, or turns on depreciation posting, only for whoever may change
  those.
