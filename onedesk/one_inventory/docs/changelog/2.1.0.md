- **Several companies.** With more than one company, every stock, buying
  and asset record shows its **Company**, starting as yours, and its list
  has a **Company** column and filter. Pickers offer only that company's
  warehouses, and Home, **Stock Summary**, **Reorder** and an item's figures
  stay your company's, in its currency. A workspace with one company looks
  as it did.
- An asset's, a warehouse's and a stock entry's figures are in their own
  company's currency. A purchase order's **Paid** counts a bill shared with
  another order once, in the order's currency. **Rejected** on a receipt
  and **Ordered** on a blanket order no longer add up different items.
- On **Stock Summary** and **Reorder**, **/** goes to the search, and on
  **Reorder** **O** drafts the purchase orders. Change them in
  **Shortcuts**. Both screens have a tour of their own, and on a tablet
  they keep the item's name readable, with its status under it.
- Ask OneAI about a warehouse or an item the way you would say it ("the
  Stores", "Concealed Hinges").
- A material request is titled by its items' names, so it reads the same
  in every language. Requests already made are retitled.
- A request for quotation printed or sent to a supplier no longer lists the
  other suppliers asked, and a supplier quoting on the portal sees their
  own currency.
- An item's last purchase price and supplier show only to whoever may read
  purchase receipts or purchase orders, and **Reorder** lists only the
  material requests you may open. **Fix** in **Inventory Check** changes the
  company's warehouses and accounts, or turns on depreciation posting, only
  for whoever may change those.
- An item's page opens quicker. Help's pages show the screens they
  describe, and say where things are now: an item's lots and reorder
  levels on its **Inventory** tab, a lot's stock levels under
  **Connections**.
- Home's figures stand two across until the screen is wide enough for
  three, so none is cut on a tablet.
- A figure with nothing in its months draws no chart.
- In OneCalendar, **Goods Expected** and **Deliveries Due** put purchase
  orders and sales orders on the day they are wanted.
- A service with no due date is no longer shown as due.
- With more than one company, **Inventory Check** names the company it checks.
- In German, an item running low still says **Knapp** while a low priority says **Niedrig**.
