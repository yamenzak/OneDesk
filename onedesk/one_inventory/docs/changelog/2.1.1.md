- An item's **Stock Value**, **On Hand** and last price are your company's,
  in its currency. An asset's, a warehouse's and a stock entry's figures are
  in their own company's currency.
- A purchase order's **Paid** counts its share of a bill shared with
  another order once, in the order's currency.
- **Rejected** on a receipt and **Ordered** on a blanket order no longer add
  up different items: they say the lines, or how far along it is.
- A request for quotation printed or sent to a supplier no longer lists the
  other suppliers asked, and a supplier quoting on the portal sees their own
  currency.
