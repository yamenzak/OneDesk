OneInventory keeps what the company holds in One: its stock, what it buys
and makes, and its equipment. This is the version it is released at.

- An item's page says how much is on hand and free to sell, what is on
  order, and what it last cost, with its stock, buying and selling a click
  away.
- Buying runs from what is low to a request for prices, the cheapest quote,
  the order and the receipt, and deliveries go out against the customer's
  order.
- The Shop Floor and Plant Floor show what the workshop is making, and work
  sent out to a supplier is followed until it comes back.
- Equipment is handed over, serviced and mended, and each piece says who
  holds it.
- Fixing what Inventory Check finds is now Fix What the Check Finds, an
  ability of the Stock Manager's that Roles and Access can give to others.
- The agreement on what OneAI reads here now says it one point to a
  sentence.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneInventory Is.
