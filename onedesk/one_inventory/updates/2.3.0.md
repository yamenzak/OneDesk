{one_inventory}'s storekeeper now drafts what suppliers send: a delivery note as a draft **Purchase Receipt** against its order, and an offer as a **Supplier Quotation**. A delivery nobody ordered waits for you to decide. Each is under the storekeeper's name, with **Undo**. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}.

{one_inventory}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
