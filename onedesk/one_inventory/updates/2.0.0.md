An item now opens on its own form, laid out like every other record: its picture, name and code at the top with **On Hand**, **Free to Sell**, **On Order** and **Stock Value** beside them, and **Order More** or **Make More** by its name. The **Inventory**, **Purchasing**, **Sales** and **Manufacturing** tabs open with what the item has there now, where it is kept, who it is bought from and at what price, who buys it and what can go now, and what it is made of, above the tab's own fields. Barcodes are kept in the item's **Barcodes** table, and a template's variants are made from **Create › Multiple Variants**.
