A work order whose steps are all done but whose goods aren't all made stays
on the Shop Floor until you finish it. A quote past its Valid Till date is
never marked Cheapest and can't be ordered, and Order the Cheapest leads the
comparison. A reorder line says what is on hand, what is projected and where
it reorders. Home offers only what you may create, follows the rail's Reports
and Setup rows, and its cards say Stores rather than Stores - SGA. A service
line counts what is still to bill, not to send. The Stock, Buying,
Manufacturing and Asset dashboards count this month and carry the currency.
