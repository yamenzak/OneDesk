**Request for Quotation** in the rail opens the list of requests, in place
of the Quotes page. On a submitted request, **View › Supplier Quotation
Comparison** sets the quotes side by side and **Create › Order Cheapest**
drafts an order to each supplier for the lines they are cheapest on.
