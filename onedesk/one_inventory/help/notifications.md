# Being Told

What {one_inventory} tells people, and when. **Notifications** in Settings
changes how each person hears of each one.

- **Delivery Late**: on the morning after a customer's order was due to be delivered and has not all gone. To whoever made the order, and the stock managers.
- **Delivery Trip Notice**: when somebody presses Notify Customers on a delivery trip, in Delivery Settings' words. To each stop's contact.
- **Depreciation Not Posted**: when the nightly job cannot post depreciation. To the role Accounts Settings names.
- **Equipment Handed Over**: when a piece of the company's equipment is given to somebody. To the person given it.
- **Expiring Soon**: when a batch with some still in stock expires within a month. To whoever keeps the stores.
- **Material Request Asked**: when somebody asks for items to be bought. To purchasing.
- **Material Request Done**: when the items you asked to be moved or issued have all been moved or issued. To whoever asked.
- **Material Request Raised**: when items fall to their reorder level and a material request is raised for them. To purchasing.
- **Material Request Received**: when what your material request asked for is received. To whoever made the request.
- **Material Request Stopped**: when somebody stops a request you made. To whoever asked.
- **Order Late**: when goods on an order are past the date they were wanted and have not all arrived. To whoever made the order.
- **Quote Received**: when a supplier sends a quote for your request. To whoever made the request, or purchasing when they cannot sign in.
- **Quote Request Sent**: when a quote request is sent to a supplier, with the link to answer it. To the supplier's contact.
- **Reordering Failed**: when reordering could not raise a request for items below their level. To the stock and buying managers.
- **Reposting Error Mail**: when stock values cannot be recalculated, mailed besides Reposting Failed, whenever the workspace sends mail. To stock managers.
- **Reposting Failed**: when stock values cannot be recalculated after a back-dated entry, so the books are behind. To stock and accounts managers.
- **Service Due**: when a service on a piece of equipment is due within a week. To whoever does the service.
- **Service Late**: when a service on a piece of equipment is past its date and not done. To whoever does the service, and the maintenance team's manager.
- **Stock Account Wrong**: when a warehouse posts to an account that is not a stock account, so stock and the books disagree. To stock and accounts managers.
- **Stock Asked**: when somebody asks for items to be moved, issued or made. To whoever keeps the stores, or makes it for a manufacture request.
- **Supplier Scorecard Standing**: when a supplier's scorecard standing changes, where the standing is set to mail. To the employee the standing names, and the supplier.
