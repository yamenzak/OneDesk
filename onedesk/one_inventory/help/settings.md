# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneInventory's settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Lists** | The values OneInventory's records pick from, such as item groups, brands and units. Each opens as its own list. |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneInventory's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who may do what in stock. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneInventory's part of the agreements. |
