OneDisplay puts what your business wants people to see on any screen with a
browser. This is the version it is released at.

- Add a TV, a kiosk or any screen by typing the code it shows, and turn it
  for a screen hung on its side.
- A channel plays slides, a layout of widgets over them, music and spoken
  ads, and its schedule changes what plays through the day, down to a
  screensaver at night.
- Every screen on a channel shows the same slide and plays the same song at
  the same moment, and keeps playing when its connection drops.
- Widgets show a queue board, a kiosk, a clock, the weather, a ticker, a
  figure or a list from your own records, laid out by dragging them on a
  live picture of the screen.
- Queues hand out tickets at a kiosk, and a counter calls them onto the board,
  read aloud in a natural voice.
- OneAI makes a picture or a whole slide from a sentence, writes a widget of
  your own, and records your spoken ads.
- Send an emergency alert to every screen at once, and hear when a screen
  goes offline.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneDisplay Is.
