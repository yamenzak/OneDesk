Screens of any size, boards, and sound where the speakers are.

- **Sound zones.** Music, voice ads and calls now belong to a sound zone, the
  place one set of speakers covers, not to a channel. Your channels' music,
  ads and calls were moved into a zone of their own, so nothing sounds
  different. A radio page plays a zone on whatever feeds the amplifier.
- **Calls from a voice pack.** A call is joined from letters, numbers and
  words OneAI says once, so a new ticket sounds as good as an old one. A
  queue chooses how its numbers are said.
- **Boards.** Keep track of anything people change by hand, rooms, bays,
  tables, a score, and show it on screens, changed from a phone.
- **Any size.** A channel can be a bar over a counter, a square, a wall of
  two screens, or any size you give it. Pictures reach each screen as sharp
  as it can show.
- **Widgets look as you set them.** Fill, colours, font, border and corners
  are set in the editor, with a **Look** to start from. Number and List are
  now text and tables with data from your records.
- **Screens report on themselves**, are marked **At Risk** before they fail,
  and start fresh every night.
- Drop pictures and videos straight onto a playlist.
