The slide designer is rebuilt as a proper editor, and what you design is
exactly what the screen shows.

- Add text, pictures, QR codes and shapes: rectangles, ellipses, triangles,
  diamonds, stars, arrows and lines.
- Choose anything and change all of it on the right: position, size and
  turn; fill, or a fade between two colours; border, corners and shadow;
  opacity and blur; font, weight, alignment across and down, spacing and
  capitals; and CSS of your own for anything else.
- Drag blocks and they settle against the slide and each other; size them
  from any side or corner; turn them with the handle above; double-click
  text to type straight onto the slide.
- Choose several at once, line them up or space them evenly, and see every
  block in Layers, where you can reorder, rename, hide and lock them.
- Undo and redo, copy and paste, duplicate, zoom, and keyboard shortcuts.
- A slide's background can fade between two colours, or be a picture you
  darken so words read over it.
