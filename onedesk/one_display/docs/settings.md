# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneDisplay's settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneDisplay's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [What You Are Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who may run the screens and send an alert. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **OneAI** | AICenter, for an administrator: what OneAI may do in OneDisplay. See [Asking OneAI](asking-oneai). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneDisplay's part of the agreements, and whether it may fetch the weather. |
