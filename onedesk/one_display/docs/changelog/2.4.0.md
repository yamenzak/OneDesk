Eighty templates instead of thirty-one, redrawn, with new groups for shops,
food and drink, clinics, offices, events and schools, each with wide, tall
and strip screens. Three new widgets came with them: Schedule (the day's
sessions with the one on now marked, or Now and Next), Opening Hours (the
week with today marked, or only whether you are open now), and a Wi-Fi way
of the QR code that a phone joins your network from.
