Presentations play on a screen the way they play in OneSlide.

- A slide with a video that does not loop stays until the video ends, then
  waits its own Advance.
- Each slide changes with its own transition: a fade, a slide in from the
  side, or a cut. Magic Move fades.
- A screen lets go of a slide's videos when the slide leaves, so an old TV
  keeps playing smoothly through a long day.
