**Edit Layout** now opens a screen in OneSlide's editor, at the screen's own
shape.

- **Add a Widget** puts one in the middle. Drag, size, line it up, restack
  and undo it as you would anything on a slide.
- Click a widget and the panel shows its source, the fields it shows, what
  it shows now and its settings.
- Put words, pictures and shapes behind the widgets: a heading, a band in
  your colour, your logo. The screens show them.
- **Preview** opens the screen as it plays.
