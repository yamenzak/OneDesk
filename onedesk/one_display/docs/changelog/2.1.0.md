Templates. A new screen opens on 28 ready-made layouts, grouped by Welcome, Queues, Boards and Rooms, Information, Promotion and Over Slides, each in your colour and with your logo, and each for the screen's shape. A channel without a layout has Choose a Layout. Add a Widget now shows every widget drawn as it will look, with 47 ready-made versions such as a corner clock, a notice bar, a door sign and a countdown, and there are 19 looks, four of them in your own colour. Widgets on the screen in the editor are drawn as they will look.
