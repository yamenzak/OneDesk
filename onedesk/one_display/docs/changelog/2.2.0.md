Widgets can now show the same thing in different ways. Choose a Layout in
the panel: the queue as one large latest call, a tile for every counter, or
the recent calls; the weather for today, by the hour, as a week list drawn
to scale, compact or as one large temperature; the clock as analog, a date
card, time and day, or time only; a ticker one message at a time or with a
label; a board as a list or a count by state; a QR code with its caption
beside it; a kiosk as tiles or rows. Add a Widget shows each one, and
three new templates use them: Every Counter, Weather and Status at a Glance.

Pairing a screen is calmer, and shows a QR code: scan it with your phone and
the Pair step opens with the code filled in.
