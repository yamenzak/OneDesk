Slides are designed on the screen itself now, and screens hear about a change
the moment it is made.

- **Design Slides** opens a designer: put words, pictures, shapes and QR
  codes on the slide, drag them into place and size them, and see it as the
  screen will. A slide OneAI designs opens there too.
- A screen hears of a change within a second: a new slide, a command, a
  ticket called at a counter.
- OneAI makes a short video for a slide, or a piece of music for a playlist.
  Either takes a few minutes, arrives paused, and you are told when it is
  there.
- A slide or a track can be **Paused**: kept in its playlist, not played.
- **Exchange Rates** is a new source: a ticker can run your currencies' rates
  along the bottom of the screen.
- A queue can tell its counters when somebody has waited too long.
- Help has pictures.
