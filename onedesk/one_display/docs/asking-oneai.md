# Asking OneAI

OneAI helps make what the screens show. It never puts anything on a screen by
itself: what it makes is kept only when you keep it.

| Ask | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Make a Picture** on a slide playlist | Describe a picture, and it comes back as a slide. |
| **Design a Slide** | Describe a slide, and it is drawn in your brand's colour, ready to change in the designer. |
| **Make a Video** on a slide playlist | Describe a short video; it is added paused, and you are told when. |
| **Make Music** on a music playlist | Describe the music; it is added paused, and you are told when. |
| **Write with OneAI** on a custom widget | Describe a widget, and its code is written for you to try. |
| **Make the Recording** on a voice ad | The ad is recorded in the voice and tone you chose. |
| Which screens are offline? | OneAI says which, and since when. |
| How is the queue? | OneAI says how many wait for each service, and who is being served. |
| Write a spoken ad about… | OneAI suggests a voice ad as a card; approving it keeps the ad. |

Each of these uses credits, and an administrator can turn OneAI off for
OneDisplay in AICenter.
