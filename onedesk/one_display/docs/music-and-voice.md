# Music and Voice

## Music

A music playlist is a list of tracks, played one after the other, over and
over, at the volume you set. Every screen on the channel plays the same part
of the same track. The music goes quiet while a call or an ad is read out.

**Make Music** has OneAI write a piece of instrumental music from a sentence.
It arrives at the end of the playlist, paused, and you are told when it is
there; clear **Paused** once you have listened to it.

## Voice Ads

A voice ad is a few sentences read aloud, in the tone and the voice you
choose, with a chime first if you like. Add it to a channel under
**Voice Ads**, to play every few minutes or at set times.

**Make the Recording** has OneAI record it, and **Listen** plays it before
any screen does. You can also upload your own recording.

## Calls Read Aloud

When a ticket is called, the queue board says it: "Ticket A 7, counter 2", in
the channel's language and voice. OneAI records each phrase the first time it
is needed and keeps it; until then the screen's own voice says it.
