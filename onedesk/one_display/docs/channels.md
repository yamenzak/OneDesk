# Channels and Schedules

A channel is what a screen plays. Many screens can play one channel, and they
stay in step with each other.

## What a Channel Plays

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Slide Playlist** | The slides, filling the screen behind everything else. See [Slides](slides). |
| **Widget Collection** | The widgets laid over the slides: a queue board, a clock, a figure from your records. See [Widgets and Sources](widgets-and-sources). |
| **Music Playlist** | The music, quietly under everything. See [Music and Voice](music-and-voice). |
| **Voice** | Whether a ticket called to a counter is said aloud, and in which language and voice. |
| **Voice Ads** | Ads read aloud every few minutes, or at set times. |

## The Schedule

Under **Schedule**, each row plays something else for part of the day: other
slides in the morning, music only at lunch, the screensaver after closing, or
the screen off at night. A row is for every day, weekdays, the weekend or one
day, between two times, and a time that crosses midnight works as you expect.
Outside every row the channel plays what is chosen above it.

**Preview** on a channel shows it as a screen shows it now, without pairing
anything.
