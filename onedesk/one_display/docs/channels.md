# Channels and Schedules

A channel is what a screen plays. Many screens can play one channel, and they
stay in step with each other.

## What a Channel Plays

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Slide Playlist** | The slides, filling the screen behind everything else. See [Slides](slides). |
| **Widget Collection** | The widgets laid over the slides: a queue board, a clock, a figure from your records. See [Widgets and Sources](widgets-and-sources). |
| **Screen Size** | The shape it is laid out for: Landscape, Portrait, a Bar over a counter, a Square, a Wall of two screens side by side, or a size of your own. A screen of another shape scales it. |
| **Language** | The language of the words on its screens. |

What people hear, music, ads and calls, is set in a sound zone. See [Sound](sound).

## The Schedule

Under **Schedule**, each row shows something else for part of the day: other
slides in the morning, other widgets at lunch, the screensaver after closing,
or the screen off at night. A row is for every day, weekdays, the weekend or one
day, between two times, and a time that crosses midnight works as you expect.
Outside every row the channel plays what is chosen above it.

**Preview** on a channel shows it as a screen shows it now, without pairing
anything.

## Nightly Reload

The screens on a channel reload at **Nightly Reload Time**, 04:00
unless you change it. An old TV's browser holds on to more the longer it
runs, and this lets it go while nobody is looking.
