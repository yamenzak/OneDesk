# Queues and Tickets

A queue is a place where people wait: its **services**, each with a letter its
tickets start with, and its **counters**, each with who usually staffs it.

## Taking a Ticket

A screen with a **Kiosk** widget shows a button for each service. Pressing
one shows the ticket's number and how many are ahead. Numbers start again
each morning.

## At the Counter

**Counter** opens on the queue and counter you staff.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Call Next** | Closes the ticket you were serving and calls whoever has waited longest. |
| **Call Again** | Calls the same ticket again, and the board says it again. |
| **Done** and **No Show** | Closes the ticket, served or not. |

Who waits, and for which service, is listed under the buttons.

## On the Board

A **Queue Board** widget shows the tickets called, newest first, and how many
wait for each service. A new call shows large for a moment and is read aloud.
