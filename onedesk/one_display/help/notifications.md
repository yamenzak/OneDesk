# What You Are Told

{one_display} tells the people who run it when something needs them.

| Notification | Who | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screen Offline** | Everybody who runs {one_display} | A screen has not been heard from for ten minutes, unless it was turned off from here. Once each time it goes quiet. |
| **Long Wait** | Whoever staffs one of the queue's counters | Somebody has waited longer than the queue allows. Once for each ticket. |
| **Past Its Time** | The board's people, or everybody who runs {one_display} when it names nobody | An item on a board has stayed in a state longer than the state allows. Once each time. |
| **Made by {one_ai}** | Whoever asked for it | A video or music {one_ai} was making is in its playlist, paused. |
| **Not Made by {one_ai}** | Whoever asked for it | {one_ai} could not make it, and nothing was added. |
| **Emergency Alert Sent** | Everybody who may send an alert, but whoever sent it | An alert went up on every screen. |

Each comes to the bell, and by email or to your phone as you choose in
NotificationCenter, from **Settings**.
