# Waiting for Agreement

**Waiting for Agreement** in {trust_center} lists what still needs somebody to
agree, then every change {one} has published to the agreements.

## What Waits

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| An agreement | You, or your organisation, haven't agreed to it yet. Open it, read it and press **Agree**. Only a Workspace Administrator agrees for the organisation. |
| **{one}'s Next Update** | Your organisation hasn't agreed to new terms, so {one}'s next update waits. It goes ahead 30 days after its window even so. |

## Changes

When {one} publishes a new version of an agreement, **Changes** lists it
with what it changes, the day it was published and the day it takes effect.
Until that day, the version you agreed to holds.

## Who Is Told

| Agreement | Who is told | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your organisation's | Every Workspace Administrator | By mail, always, and in the bell |
| Your own | Everybody in the workspace | In the bell |

Nobody is stopped at sign-in for a new version. An organisation's agreement
takes effect at least 30 days after it is published.

> **To object to a new subprocessor, reply before the day it takes effect.**
> The Data Processing Addendum gives you until then.
