# Changes

When {one} publishes a new version of an agreement, **Changes** in {trust_center}
lists it with what it changes, the day it was published and the day it takes
effect. Until that day, the version you agreed to holds.

## Who Is Told

| Agreement | Who is told | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your organisation's | Every Workspace Administrator | By mail, always, and in the bell |
| Your own | Everybody in the workspace | In the bell |

Nobody is stopped at sign-in for a new version. An organisation's agreement
takes effect at least 30 days after it is published.

> **To object to a new subprocessor, reply before the day it takes effect.**
> The Data Processing Addendum gives you until then.
