# Agreeing

Some agreements are your organisation's, and an administrator agrees to
them for everybody. Others are about your own personal data, so only you
can agree to them.

## Who Agrees to What

| Agreement | Who agrees |
| --- | --- |
| **Terms of Service**, **Data Processing Addendum**, **Subprocessors**, **AI Addendum** | A Workspace Administrator, once, for the organisation. |
| **Privacy Policy**, **Cookie Policy** | Each person, for themselves. |
| **Acceptable Use Policy** | Both: the organisation promises it, and each person agrees to it. |

## When You're Asked

The first time you sign in, One asks before you carry on. Each agreement
opens in a new tab to read. Tick **I agree to these** and press **Agree**.

If your organisation's agreements still wait for an administrator, One
says so and opens once they have agreed.

## Where You Stand

{trust_center} shows, at the top of each agreement, whether **You** and **Your
Organisation** agreed, who did and when. **Agree** is there while one is
owed, and earlier versions open from their buttons beside it.

> **What is kept is more than a yes.** It is the agreement, its exact
> version, the time, the account and the network address it came from.
