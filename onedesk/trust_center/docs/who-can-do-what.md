# Who Can Do What

Everybody can read every agreement and agree to their own. What binds the
organisation is a Workspace Administrator's, who can do everything.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Read the agreements and agree to their own. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | See who in the workspace agreed to each agreement, and when. |

## What Stays With Administrators

Agreeing for the organisation and turning an optional service on or off
are always a Workspace Administrator's.
