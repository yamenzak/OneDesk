- **Requests** is a page of its own under Your Data, with how many are
  waiting beside it, so an administrator sees a copy or a deletion someone
  asked for without looking.
- **Companies** shows each company as a card: what it receives and where.
  The ones your workspace does not use are greyed rather than struck out.
- Help and Enlarge are in the dialog's top corner, so the sidebar keeps its
  width for the names.
