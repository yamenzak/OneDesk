{trust_center}'s sections are now in this order: the agreements, **Companies That Receive Data**, **Optional Services**, **Your Data**, **Privacy Requests**, then **Waiting for Agreement**. **Waiting for Agreement**, which was **Changes**, lists each agreement you or your organisation haven't agreed to yet, and {one}'s next update while it waits for your organisation's agreement, above every change to the agreements.

Its tile in {one_hub} says what it is for.

TrustCenter's two roles take its name: **TrustCenter User**, which everyone holds, and **TrustCenter Manager**, which sees **Who Agreed**. Whoever held them before holds them still, and the agreements are the ones you agreed to.

When you hold a request to delete someone's data, the box for the reason says it is what they are told.

The Terms of Service say a plan includes credits each month, in plain words. Nothing they say has changed.

**Training Copies** is offered under **Optional Services**, and in the setup wizard, only when your plan includes it.

{trust_center} opens in the same frame as every centre; its **Search** finds words on every page as before.

In Arabic, an agreement's English text reads left to right, so its full stops end its lines again.
