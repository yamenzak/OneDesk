# Sending a Link

**Portal**, on a submitted quotation, order, shipment, invoice, quote
request, quote, purchase order or purchase invoice, has **Send Link** at
the top.

- **Send Link** sends a link to the record to one person, who needs no
  account: a contact of the customer or supplier, or **Someone else…** by
  email address. The copy button beside it makes the link without sending
  it and copies it.
- A person has one open link per record, so sending again sends the same
  one.
- It opens the record until its day: a month, or a quotation's own last
  day if that is sooner.
- Through it they can accept or decline a quotation, or price and send a
  quote, once, giving their name. After that it only shows the record and
  who answered. Opening it does not use it up, so a mail scanner that opens
  it first spends nothing.

Under **Links** is each link that still opens or was answered, with how
often it was opened, and **Copy**, **Email Again**, **Rotate** (a new link
in its place; the old one stops) and **Revoke**. A link that is revoked,
expired or mistyped says it no longer works, and nothing else.

## Who Sees One Record

Under **People**, everybody of the customer or supplier who signs in is
**Can see** or **Hidden** for this record. By default everyone sees every
record of their customer or supplier. Hidden, the record no longer lists,
opens or prints for them, so two departments of one customer can each see
their own. Only who is hidden is kept, so a contact invited later sees it.
Projects, timesheets and addresses stay the whole company's.
