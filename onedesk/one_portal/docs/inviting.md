# Inviting Someone

Open a customer, a supplier or one of their contacts and click **Portal**.
Each of their contacts is listed with their email and where they stand:
not on the portal, invited, when they last signed in, or that their access
is turned off.

- **Invite** sends them an email with a link to choose their password,
  which works for a week. They then sign in with their email address. A
  contact needs an email to be invited.
- **Invite Again**, under **⋯**, sends a new link to somebody who has not
  signed in yet. The old one stops working.
- **Revoke Access**, under **⋯**, turns their login off and signs them out
  everywhere at once.
- **Restore** turns it back on. Somebody who never signed in is sent a new
  link.

On a contact, the line across the top says the same: invited, signed in, or
access revoked.

## Who Can Invite

A workspace administrator, whoever holds **Invite to the Portal**, and, for
their own contacts, a customer's manager in sales or a supplier's manager in
buying. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what).
