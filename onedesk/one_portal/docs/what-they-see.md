# What They See

- **A customer** sees their quotations, orders, invoices, shipments,
  addresses, projects and timesheets.
- **A supplier** sees the quote requests sent to them, their quotes, and
  their purchase orders and purchase invoices.

Each sees only the records of their own customer or supplier. Home has a
tile for each kind they have, with what is left to pay on invoices; **Needs
You**, the records that ask something of them (an invoice to pay, a quotation
to review, a quote request to answer), late ones first; and **Recent**, the
last five. A list has its states as tabs with their counts (All, Overdue,
Partly Paid), and each row says what it is for and the date it looks ahead
to: when an invoice is due or how many days overdue, until when a quotation
holds, when an order is delivered. On a
computer the tabs are in a sidebar under their company's name; on a phone
Home and the first three tabs are a bar along the bottom, and **More** has
the rest. Which tabs show is set in Portal Settings. Each list has a
search, and **Load More** under it once there are more than twenty.

Opening a record shows where it is on its way (an order: Confirmed,
Delivered, Invoiced, Paid), its items and totals, and beside them what it
comes to: what is left to pay on an invoice and when it is due, or a
quotation's total and until when it holds. Under that are its details and
the records before and after it (the quotation an order came from, its
shipments and invoices). **Download PDF** gives the printed copy. Each sees
only their own records.

## A Supplier's Quote

A supplier answers a quote request on the portal: they set a rate for each
item they can supply, change the quantity if they need to, give a discount on
any row, add notes, and choose **Send Quote**. As they type, each row shows
its tax and the quote its taxes and total, the same as once it is sent. They
cannot change the tax: it is the workspace's own (the purchase taxes template,
an item's Item Tax Template, a Tax Rule by the supplier's country), so it
lands in the right accounts. Their quote lands in Quotes, and whoever made the
request is told (Quote Received). A request they have answered says Quoted.

## A Customer Accepting

A customer answers a quotation that is still open on its page. **Accept
Quotation** asks when they need it by and their own reference, and makes the
order from the quotation the way the team would on the desk: a draft, for
whoever made the quotation to check and submit. They are told (Quotation
Accepted), and the quotation says Accepted until the order is confirmed.
**Decline** asks why, if they want to say, sets the quotation lost with that
reason, and tells whoever made it (Quotation Declined); the customer sees it as
Declined. An open quotation is on Home under Needs You until it is answered.

A customer sees the addresses you keep for them, and adds and changes their
own (**Add Address**). They follow their projects: how far along each is,
when it ends, its tasks and its order and invoices. Timesheets show the
hours worked for them, newest first, one row per day's timesheet.

An invoice paid in parts shows each part still to pay with its own due date.

## Their Own Account

Their own pages are under **Account** in the sidebar (under **More** on a
phone):

- **Profile**: their name, picture, phone, language and time zone, saved with
  **Save** in the page's head.
- **Password**: their current password, then the new one twice, and
  **Update**. It says how strong the new one is as they type, and can sign
  them out of every other device.
- **Your Data**: **Request My Data** asks for a copy, which an administrator
  reviews first; when it is ready, **Download My Data**. **Delete My
  Account** asks for the account to be deleted, confirmed with their
  password, and an administrator decides (TrustCenter › Your Data › Requests).
  **Cancel Deletion** withdraws it until then.
- **Third Party Apps**: shown once they have let an app sign in for them.
  **Revoke** signs the app out.
