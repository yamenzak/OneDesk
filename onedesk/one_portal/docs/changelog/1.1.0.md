What your customers and suppliers see on the portal reads better.

- An invoice paid in parts shows each part still to pay with its own due
  date, so what is late and what is due later no longer disagree.
- Addresses shows the addresses you keep for a customer, beside the ones
  they add themselves.
- Timesheets are listed newest first, each by its day.
- On a phone, Home keeps money whole and opens a record from its row.
- People in OnePortal shows where each person's sign-in stands.
- A link to one record is now in the Data Processing Addendum.
