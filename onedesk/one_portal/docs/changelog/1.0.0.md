The portal your customers and suppliers sign in to now has its own Help,
and one window to run it from.

- **Portal**, on a customer, a supplier, a contact or a sent record, lists
  every contact with where they stand: not on the portal, invited, signed
  in, or turned off.
- Invite someone, invite them again, turn their access off or back on,
  right where they are listed.
- On a sent record, the same window sends a link to anybody with an email
  address and says who of the customer or supplier can see it.
- Whoever holds **Invite to the Portal** can invite any customer's or
  supplier's contact.
- OnePortal is on the rail, with **People**, every contact and where they
  stand, and **Links**, every link sent. Turned off in Apps, the portal
  closes and every Portal button goes.
