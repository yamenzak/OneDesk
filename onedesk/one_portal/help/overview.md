# The Portal

Your customers and suppliers can see their own records on the portal,
without being people in your workspace. There are two ways in.

- **An account.** You invite a customer's or supplier's contact. They choose
  a password and sign in, and from then on see every record of their own
  customer or supplier. See [Inviting Someone](inviting).
- **A link.** You send a link to one record to anybody with an email
  address. It opens that record and nothing else, without an account, until
  its day. See [Sending a Link](links).

Both are done from one window, **Portal**, on a customer, a supplier, a
contact, or a submitted quotation, order, shipment, invoice, quote request,
quote or purchase document. What each customer and supplier sees there is
in [What They See](what-they-see).

**{one_portal}** on the rail lists the same two things: **People**, everybody
invited to the portal and where their login stands, and **Links**, every link
sent and whether it is open, answered, expired or revoked.

Nobody can sign up on their own, and somebody who signs in to your
workspace signs in to {one}, not the portal. An administrator can turn the
whole portal off in {one_marketplace}.
