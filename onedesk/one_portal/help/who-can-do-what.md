# Who Can Do What

Everybody can open **Portal** on a record they can read and see who of its
customer or supplier is on the portal. Inviting and turning access off are
for fewer people.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | See who is on the portal, and send a link to a record they may change. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Invite any customer's or supplier's contact, and turn their access off. |

## What Each Role Starts With

This is what OnePortal's roles hold when the portal arrives in a workspace.
**Roles** in AccessCenter changes any of it, and an update never undoes a
change you made.

## Without the Role

A Workspace Administrator can do everything. A customer's manager in sales
can invite that customer's contacts, and a supplier's manager in buying that
supplier's, without OnePortal's Manager.
