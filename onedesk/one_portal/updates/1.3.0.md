**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_portal} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

Signing up for {one} on {one_admin}'s site is drawn by the portal: one question a screen, with Back, Continue and a bar for how far you are. Nothing changes for the people who sign in to your portal.

Behind the scenes, the portal follows the new names of {one_deck}, {trust_center} and {account_center}; nothing else changes for you.

In Arabic, the portal's pages read right to left on a computer as they do on a phone.

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.

{one_portal} has a page in {one_marketplace}, for administrators: what it does, what it brings, whether your plan includes it and the credits it uses.
