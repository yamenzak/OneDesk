When a quality inspection is submitted as **Rejected**, {one_quality}'s quality inspector now raises it as a non conformance, with the readings that failed. Where it cannot tell which procedure it is against, it suggests it to you instead. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}. The AI Addendum now says so.

{one_quality}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
