# Asking {one_ai}

The {one_ai} panel offers questions that fit the page you are on. Press one, or
type your own.

- **Home**: **What needs fixing?**, the open problems, the steps overdue
  and with whom, and the goals failing.
- **A non conformance or an action**: **What was done about it?** and
  **What is still open?**
- **A procedure**: **Summarise this procedure** and **What is still open?**

{one_ai} reads quality records as you see them and changes none of them.

## The Quality Inspector

When a quality inspection is submitted as **Rejected**, the quality
inspector, {one_quality}'s agent, raises it as an open non conformance with
the readings that failed and the inspection's remarks. Where it cannot tell
which procedure the problem is against, it suggests the non conformance as a
card for whoever submitted the inspection. How far it goes is set on its tab
in {ai_center}.
