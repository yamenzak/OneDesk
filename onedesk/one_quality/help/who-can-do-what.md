# Who Can Do What

A **Quality User** writes procedures, goals and reviews, raises non
conformances and works quality actions, meetings and feedback. A **Quality
Manager** also deletes them and keeps the feedback templates.

**Resolve Non Conformances** is for the managers to begin with: it sets a
non conformance to **Resolved**. **Roles** in AccessCenter gives it to other
roles.
