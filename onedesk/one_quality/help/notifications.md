# Being Told

What {one_quality} tells people, and when. **Notifications** in Settings
changes how each person hears of each one.

- **Non Conformance Raised**: when somebody records work that did not follow its procedure. To the quality managers.
- **Non Conformance Resolved**: when a non conformance somebody raised is resolved. To whoever raised it.
- **Quality Step Overdue**: the day after a step of a quality action was due and is still open. To whoever is responsible for the step.
