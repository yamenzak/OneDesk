**OneQuality** is a new app in the dock for how the company keeps its work right: **Quality Procedure**, **Quality Goal** and **Quality Review**, **Non Conformance** and **Quality Action**, **Quality Meeting** and **Quality Feedback**, with the **Review** report. They were under Quality in OneInventory. Anybody can record a non conformance, the quality managers are told at once, and only somebody who may **Resolve Non Conformances** sets one to Resolved. People gives OneQuality as a user or a manager.
