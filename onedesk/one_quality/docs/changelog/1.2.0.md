OneQuality now opens on **Home**: the non conformances still open, the actions open, the steps overdue and the goals failing, each listed under **Today**. **Report a Problem** at the top records a non conformance, from a phone on site too.

- On a non conformance, **Quality Action** makes an action for it, and the **Actions** card says how many are done. An action shows the problem it fixes.
- If you are responsible for a step of an action, you are told the morning after it was due. If you raised a problem, you are told when it is resolved.
- A non conformance is called by its subject, shows its process owner by name, and no longer asks for a status when you report one.
- On Home, **N** reports a problem and **A** starts a quality action. Ask OneAI **What needs fixing?** there.
- With more than one company, a non conformance and a quality action belong to one, and Home shows your company's.
