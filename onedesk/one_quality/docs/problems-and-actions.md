# Problems and Actions

When work did not follow its procedure, anybody can record a **Non
Conformance**, not only the people in quality: what happened, against which procedure, and what should be
done to put it right (**Corrective Action**) and to stop it happening again
(**Preventive Action**). The quality managers are told at once.

On a site, press **Report a Problem** on Home, give it a subject and its
procedure, and add photos in **Details** with its picture button, which on a
phone takes one with the camera.

A **Quality Action** is what is done about a problem: corrective or
preventive, the procedure and goal it is for, the non conformance, review
or feedback it came from, and each resolution with whoever carries it out
and by when. It is **Open** until it is **Completed**. On a non
conformance, **Quality Action** with a + makes one for it, and the
**Actions** card at the top says how many are done. Whoever is responsible
for a step is told the morning after it was due, and whoever raised the
problem is told when it is resolved.

Once its actions are done, a non conformance is set to **Resolved**. Only
somebody who may resolve non conformances can do that, a quality manager to
begin with. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what).
