# Procedures and Goals

A **Quality Procedure** says how a piece of work is done, step by step, and
who owns it (**Process Owner**). A procedure can hold others, so a big job
is broken into smaller ones.

A **Quality Goal** says what the business means to achieve when it follows a
procedure: each objective with its target and unit, such as 98 percent of
bays passing the water test the first time. It also says how often it is
checked.

Each check is a **Quality Review**: every objective with what was achieved
against its target, and whether the review **Passed** or **Failed**. A failed
review is followed up with a **Quality Action**. See [Problems and
Actions](problems-and-actions).
