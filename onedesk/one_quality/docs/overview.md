# What OneQuality Is

OneQuality is how the company keeps its work right: how each piece of work
is done, the goals it is held to, what went wrong, and what is done about
it. It suits a business that works to procedures, such as installing a
curtain wall or fitting a kitchen, and wants every slip written down and
followed until it is fixed.

## Finding Your Way

**OneQuality** in the dock opens it on **Non Conformance**. The rail keeps
the rest:

- **Non Conformance** and **Quality Action**: what went wrong and what is
  done about it. See [Problems and Actions](problems-and-actions).
- **Quality Procedure**, **Quality Goal** and **Quality Review**: how work is
  done and how well. See [Procedures and Goals](procedures-and-goals).
- **Quality Meeting** and **Quality Feedback**. See [Meetings and
  Feedback](meetings-and-feedback).
- **Reports**: **Review**, every action with the review it came from.
- **Setup**: the questions feedback asks.

## What Is Kept Where

Checking goods on arrival or while they are made is a **Quality
Inspection**, kept in OneInventory with the stock it checks.
