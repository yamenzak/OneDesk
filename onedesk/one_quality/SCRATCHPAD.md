# OneQuality scratchpad

What is known to be wrong and what is planned, kept short.

- **A Home.** It opens on Non Conformance. A Home would say what is open,
  what is overdue and which goals are failing.
- **The site job.** Report a snag or a non conformance from a phone on site,
  with photos, and turn it into an action with tasks and an owner
  (docs/BACKLOG.md, OneQuality).
- **The walk.** First walk 5 November: heads and strips declared, walked at
  1440 and 390 in Arabic and dark for a non conformance, a goal and the
  actions list. A procedure's and a goal's tables cut their steps (frappe's
  grid, for OneInterface). A non conformance's Process Owner is a plain
  text field ERPNext fills with an address.
