With OneCloud connected as a drive on your computer, your presentations show
there as PowerPoint files. Open one in PowerPoint, Keynote or LibreOffice,
save, and the changes come back into the same presentation, with what it was
before kept in **Version History**. A chart, SmartArt or a video can't come
back, and the next time you open the presentation you are told which slides
had one.

In OneCloud, **Edit** on a presentation also offers **Edit in OneOS**, which
saves back the same way, and **Download as PowerPoint**.
