Five finished decks and a slide library.

- **New › Presentation** offers a pitch, a project update, a tender
  response, a monthly review and a training session, each in your language
  and your business's colour and logo.
- **Add Slide › Slide Library** has a finished slide of every kind (title,
  agenda, section, two columns, picture and text, quote, team, timeline,
  figures, pricing, thank you) and your company's own, which a manager adds
  with **Save to Library** on a slide's menu.
