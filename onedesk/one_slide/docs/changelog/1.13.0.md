Your phone can now be the remote, and point at the slide.

- In the presenter view, Phone Remote shows a code. Scan it with your
  phone's camera and the phone moves the slides, shows the slide's notes and
  the time, with no app and no signing in.
- Keep a finger on Hold to Point and turn the phone: a red dot follows on the
  slide. A phone without a motion sensor gets a pad to drag instead.
- The presenter view can Present Live too, so guests on a link follow slides
  moved there, from the keyboard or the phone.
- The remote ends when the presentation does.
