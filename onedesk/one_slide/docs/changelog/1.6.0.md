A master under every slide, and slides that follow their layout.

- **File › Edit Master** opens what every slide draws underneath its own: a
  logo, a footer, the slide number, the date. **Show the Master**, in a
  slide's panel, leaves it off that one slide. Screens and PDFs draw it too,
  and a template brings its master with it.
- A slide added from a template remembers its layout. When the template's
  look changes, **File › Restyle from Layouts** brings it into every such
  slide and keeps your words.
