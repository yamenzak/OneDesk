OneAI can write a whole deck, and change one slide at a time.

- **OneAI › Write a Whole Deck** asks what the deck is for. OneAI reads the
  record the presentation is on and shows the slides it would write as a
  card. Nothing changes until you approve it. The slides are built in your
  business's colour, and values from records stay up to date.
- **Rewrite**, **Shorten**, **Translate** and **Make This Slide a Timeline**
  work on the slide that is open, the same way.
- OneAI's panel now knows the presentation and the slide you are on.
