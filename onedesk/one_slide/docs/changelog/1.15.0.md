Things on a slide can come and go.

- **Motion.** Anything on a slide can fade, fly, zoom or rise in, stay a
  while and leave, with the slide or on a click, once or over and over. The
  presenter view, the phone remote and shared links step through it.
- **Plays Sound** on a video lets it play its own sound on a screen.
- On a screen, a widget's look is set in the panel like anything else.
