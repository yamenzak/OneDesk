A presentation as a PowerPoint file you can edit.

- **File › Export as PowerPoint** keeps a .pptx beside the presentation:
  words keep their font, size, colour and weight, shapes stay shapes, and
  pictures, videos, tables, backgrounds, the master and speaker notes come
  along. It opens in PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides.
- A blur, free CSS or a widget has nothing like it in PowerPoint, so it is
  left out, and you are told which slides lost one.
