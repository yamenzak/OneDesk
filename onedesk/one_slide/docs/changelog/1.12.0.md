A presentation shared by a OneCloud link is played on the link's page.

- Whoever opens the link sees the slides, moves with the arrows and can fill
  their screen. Words from your records show as you see them, so nobody on
  the link reaches the records. If the link allows downloading, they get it
  as PowerPoint.
- **Present ▾ › Present Live**: everyone with the link follows your slides,
  and someone who wanders off can follow you again.
- **File › Views**: who looked, when, for how long, and how long each slide
  held them. The privacy policy says what is recorded.
- Tables drawn on a screen show again, and a master's date no longer wraps.
