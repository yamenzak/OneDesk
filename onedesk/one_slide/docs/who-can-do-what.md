# Who Can Do What

A presentation is a file in OneCloud, so whoever may open the file may open
the presentation, and whoever may change the file may change it. Sharing it
is sharing the file.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Make presentations, and work on the ones shared with them. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also keep a presentation as a template for everyone. |

## What Each Role Starts With

## Without the Role

A Workspace Administrator can do everything, and changes or deletes any
template.
