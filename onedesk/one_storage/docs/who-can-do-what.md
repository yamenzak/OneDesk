# Who Can Do What

OneCloud has no roles of its own. Who may open or change a file follows from
where it is and who it was shared with, and everybody on the team uses
OneCloud.

## Who Can Open a File

| Where the file is | Who can open it | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My Files** | You, and whoever you share it with | You, and whoever you share it with to edit |
| **Company** | Everybody on the team | Whoever added it, and workspace administrators |
| A library | Its members | Its Members and Owners |
| A record's folder | Whoever can open the record | Whoever can change the record |
| A server under **Network** | Whoever connected it, or everybody when an administrator shares it | The same people |

Everybody on the team can add files to Company. A person can add papers to
their own employee record's folder.

## Who Can Share

Whoever can change a file or a folder can share it and make a link to it:
its owner, a workspace administrator, or somebody it was shared with to
edit. A file on a record is shared by sharing the record. See
[Sharing With the Team](sharing-with-the-team) and
[Sharing Outside the Team](sharing-outside).

## Workspace Administrators

A workspace administrator can rename, move and delete anything in Company,
open and change every library, and is the only one who can share a server
connection with the whole team.

> Nobody sees another person's My Files, an administrator included, unless
> it was shared with them.
