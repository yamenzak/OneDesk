**OneOS**, under **Activity**, lists every file a workspace's people have open in OneOS, with the workspace, the person, its status and the minutes billed. Each minute a session runs is charged to its workspace in OneCredits. A session stops by itself after 15 minutes without use, after 8 hours, or when the workspace can't cover the next 30 minutes, and its **Stop Reason** says which. **Stop Session** on a session closes it, keeping what was saved.

The price list's menu has **OneCredit Rates**: what a conversion to PDF, a mail and a minute of OneOS cost in OneCredits.

A workspace in its trial isn't offered OneOS, and no session is started for it.

**Set Up Cloudflare** now also sets up OneOS, and shows its address as **OneOS** under **Details**. Press **Set Up Again** once its desktop has been built.

**Connect GitHub** now also lets OneAdmin write the repository's secrets, so nobody adds one on GitHub. Production writes the release secrets when the app is installed, and **Set Up Again** writes the ones the OneOS desktop is built with. The **GitHub** tile under **Last Setup** shows them, and the **AWS and GitHub** tab lists what was written.
