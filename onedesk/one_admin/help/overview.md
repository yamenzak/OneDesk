# What {one_admin} Is

{one_admin} is the console {one} is run from. It holds the workspaces customers pay
for, the servers they run on, the jobs that build and close them, their
domains, the price list, signups and credits. Only operators see it, and only
on the admin site.

## Finding Your Way

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **The rail**, on the left | **Home**, **Workspaces**, **Servers** and **Status** (whether every service is working, with its history), then **Activity** (jobs, the log, domains, the files open in {oneos}), **Billing** (the price list, signups, credits) and **{one_ai}** (what AI costs). |
| **Home** | The figures for the business with their charts (revenue, signups, workspaces, {one_ai}, servers, health and releases), and what needs you, most pressing first. |
| **The foot** | **Settings**, the play button that takes the tour, the question mark that opens this Help, and {one_admin}'s version, which opens What's New. |

See [Workspaces](workspaces) for what happens to one, and [Settings](settings)
for what {one_admin} runs on.

> A workspace's people never see {one_admin}, whatever roles they hold there.
