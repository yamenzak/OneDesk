Each signup in **Account Request** shows its **Field**, what the company does as the setup asked, and the list filters by it. Choosing a field in the setup now also fills in that field's working day and its suggested answers for each app's setup, which the person can still change.

A session started from **Demo Data** now opens in about a minute: each release brings the demo company ready-made, and the session is made from it instead of building it. A release without one builds the demo as before, which takes a while. The session's timeline says which it was, and why when it was built.

A workspace's administrators now start training copies themselves. Each shows in **Workspace Session** with who started it and, from the demo, its **Field**, and the workspace's administrator signs in to it rather than an operator. A copy of a workspace's data now has each person's password cleared.

The privacy policy and the subprocessors list now say what each kind of training copy holds, that a copy of your data has its passwords cleared, and that your administrators open and end copies themselves.
