Each record in **Private Apps** now takes its title, sentence and icon from the private app's own manifest, which says what the app is the way each of {one}'s apps does. A release refuses a private app with no manifest, or one that names an app {one} doesn't have. A record from before keeps what it showed until the next release brings it up to date; you don't need to do anything.

Anything that changes a record now happens only when you press its button, never when a link is opened by accident.

Each plan names what it includes beyond its quotas: **Training Copies** and the demo company on every plan, a server of its own on Business and Scale. A workspace keeps what its plan had when it bought it, and is told it with the rest of its account. A training copy starts only for a workspace whose plan includes **Training Copies** and that has them on, and a copy is deleted when its plan no longer includes them. Every workspace keeps what it could do before.

Behind the scenes, {one_admin} follows the new names of {one_cloud}, {trust_center} and {account_center}; nothing else changes for you.
