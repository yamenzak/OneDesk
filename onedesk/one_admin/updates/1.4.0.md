**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_admin} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

Each record in **Private Apps** now takes its title, sentence and icon from the private app's own manifest, which says what the app is the way each of {one}'s apps does. A release refuses a private app with no manifest, or one that names an app {one} doesn't have. A record from before keeps what it showed until the next release brings it up to date; you don't need to do anything.

Anything that changes a record now happens only when you press its button, never when a link is opened by accident.

A server you connected can be let go with **Release Server** on its record. It won't while a workspace is on it, and says which. Its sessions are deleted, and you run one line on the machine that removes what **Connect Server** put there and lists what it removed. The server then reads **Released**, or press **Finish Release** if the machine couldn't say so. The row stays, a server can be deleted only once it is released, and **Connect Again** brings the same machine back.

Behind the scenes, {one_admin} follows the new names of {one_cloud}, {trust_center} and {account_center}; nothing else changes for you.

Amounts of {one_credits} read as plain credits: Home's **Credits This Month** and **Credits Used by Month**, Billing's **Credits Sold This Month** and **Credits Used This Month**, a credit pack's name (**1,000 Credits**), a workspace's **Monthly Limit (Credits)** and a {oneos} session's **Credits Charged** and **Rate (Credits per Minute)**.

Each plan names what it includes beyond its quotas: **Training Copies** and the demo company on every plan, a server of its own on Business and Scale. A workspace keeps what its plan had when it bought it, and is told it with the rest of its account. A training copy starts only for a workspace whose plan includes **Training Copies** and that has them on, and a copy is deleted when its plan no longer includes them. Every workspace keeps what it could do before.

A new workspace's build has a step after **Configuring the site**: **Setting up the workspace**, which sets the site up from the signup, in its country, time zone and currency, with the workspace's name as its company and the owner as its administrator. A setup that fails stops the build with the site's reason, and **Resume** carries on from where it stopped. A signup whose country can't be told from its time zone is left to the setup wizard on the site, as before.

**Price Check** and **Plan Calculator** have an **Agent Cost** column: what {one_ai}'s agents cost of each plan, add-on and credit pack on Cloudflare, already counted in **Cost** and **Margin**. Plan Calculator's summary also shows the agents' monthly cost for a workspace of the seats you enter. The **20,000 Credits** pack is AED 769, so that it keeps the margin once agents are counted; a credit is still worth AED 0.04.

**Set Up Cloudflare** also deploys the Worker every app's agent runs on, and **One Admin Settings** shows its address under **Agents**.

Signing up is a setup that asks one question a screen, on a phone as on a computer: who you are, the company and its country, its language, currency and time zone, what it does, any other companies, its apps (turned on to suit what it does, with what each needs), the plan and where data is kept, then payment. While the workspace is built it asks the working week, each app's own questions and who to invite, and the workspace is set up from the answers. Each **Account Request** keeps them under **Setup**, with every step reached, finished, skipped or left, and its lead and deal say where it left or that it finished. **Signups** on **Home** shows how far people get and where unpaid signups stop. The privacy policy says what is kept of what is typed into the signup, and the terms say that continuing from its first page is when you agree.

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.

A workspace is told whether its update is under way and, while it hasn't agreed to new terms, the day it updates even so, which its {update_center} and {trust_center} show.
