**ShortcutCenter** is new, under **Centers** on One's rail. It lists every key One answers to, app by app, and where each one works. Change any of them by pressing the keys or clicking them on the keyboard and mouse pictures; what you change is yours alone. **Shift+/** opens it.

One's own: **Alt+Shift+H** goes Home, **Alt+Shift+N** opens your notifications, **Ctrl+Shift+G** changes the appearance.
