- One no longer syncs contacts or calendars with a Google account. The
  Google fields are gone from contacts and events, and nobody can set up
  the sync. Your calendar link still adds your calendar to Google Calendar.
- OneAI knows what AccessCenter, TrustCenter, HelpCenter and UpdateCenter
  are when you ask it from one of them.
- A list of things you can tick in the left pane, like OneCalendar's
  calendars, shows each box in its own colour.
