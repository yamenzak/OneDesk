A large export now waits for an administrator: more records at once than the workspace's **Export Limit**, from a list, a report, Data Export or several files at once. The person asking sees that it waits, an administrator presses **Approve Export** on Home, and the person exports it again within a day. Set the limit under **Sharing** in {control_center}. **Waiting for Your Approval** on Home also lists the payments and bank details waiting for you as a second person. Where nobody else can approve, you approve your own and say why. The privacy policy now says what each such request keeps: who asked, why, who decided and their remarks, with a bank account number shown only by its last four digits.

A new workspace starts set up for what its company does. Besides the apps its field turns on, it gets that field's working day, such as eight in the morning to eight at night for a clinic, with the days off its country keeps, and a clinic keeps mail to the clinic's own mailboxes. Whatever the owner answered when signing up comes first.

If you administer the workspace, you can make a training copy of it yourself, under **Training Copies** in {account_center}, on a plan that includes it once Training Copies is on in Optional Services. **Fresh** is set up like this workspace with no records, **From the Demo** opens on the company of the field you choose, and **From Our Data** is last night's backup with each person's password cleared. **Open** signs you in to a copy, and **End** deletes it.

On Home, what waits for you from {one_ai} counts what the agents did for you as well as what {one_ai} read.

Each centre has a mark of its own now, in {one}'s ring: a question mark for {help_center}, a tick for {setup_center}, four squares for {one_hub}, and {one_credits} in a gold ring. {one_marketplace} has a mark too, and {one_chat}, {one_doc}, {one_sheet}, {one_deck}, {one_factory}, {one_portal} and {one_quality} have new ones.

If you administer the workspace, **Branding** in {control_center} now has its **Workspace Name** and **Workspace Logo** beside the **Brand Colour** and **Frame Colour**, and the Brand Colour also colours the main buttons on every screen. The **Company Logo** for printed documents is under **Documents**.

In {setup_center}, **Ask** beside a step puts it to {setup_center}'s setup guide in {one_ai}'s panel, which says what the step sets up and what to answer. Ask it to fill the step in and the answers wait on a card: nothing is saved until you press **Approve**.

{automation_center} is new, under Centers in {one}'s rail: what happens by itself, step by step. Each mailbox whose rules you keep or ask for has its list there, in the order they run, and so does each channel of {one_chat}'s you are in, if your role lets you make channels. Opening one draws it as a flow, from **When a message arrives** to each step on its right, an **If** splitting into **Then** and **Otherwise**; pick a step to change it beside the flow, or press the plus to add one. **New Automation** starts from one of the usual ones, such as **Acknowledge New Mail**.

In {automation_center}, an automation opens with all its steps in view, and you zoom and move around it.
