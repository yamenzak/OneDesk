Adding a news source starts from what you have: paste a site's address and see its newest headlines before adding it, or pick several feeds near you from the library at once. Fetched headlines show as a read-only article with its picture, and Hide Headline keeps one off the top bar and every screen. A weather place is picked from a list as you type, or filled in from where you are with the location button. Short forms open in a small dialog. The agreements change: the privacy notice now says that news pictures load from their publishers, and what the location button uses.
