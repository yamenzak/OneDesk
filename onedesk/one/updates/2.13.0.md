The rail is yours. **Arrange Apps**, in your menu and at the foot of **Apps**, puts the apps in your own order and into folders you name, and **Reset** goes back to the workspace's order. Your order and folders stay through updates.

**Apps**, at the bottom of the rail, is for everybody now: your apps with the ones you opened last first, and a search that finds apps and the pages inside them. On a phone, **Apps** in the tab bar is the same. Administrators turn apps on and off from **Turn Apps On or Off** at its foot, and set the order of the apps for everybody in {setup_center}, under **Order of Apps**.

An app you have no role in isn't on your rail, in **Apps**, in search or in Help, and a centre you can't open isn't in {one_hub} or the arrow's list.

{one_hub} holds every centre you may open as a tile, with what each is for and a search over them. The mark at the top of the rail opens it, and so does **{one_hub}** under **Centers** on {one}'s rail. A centre opened from any screen opens over it, with a way up to {one_hub} above its name. The arrow at the right of the top bar lists the same centres, on a computer and on a phone, with {control_center} first on {one}'s screens. The foot of {one}'s rail keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

{setup_center}, under **Centers** on {one}'s rail, lists what each app needs set up and which of it is done. A step asked when the workspace was set up has the same fields here, and **Set Up** opens any other in its app's Settings. An app's **Getting Started** opens it, and so does turning an app on in **Apps**. Search finds every centre too.

Amounts of {one_credits} read as plain credits, such as "120 credits": Home's **Credits** tile, **Credits are running low**, the tour and {one_ai}'s suggested questions say credits, in English, Arabic and German.

The Terms of Service say a plan includes credits each month, in plain words. Nothing they say has changed.

A document made in {one_doc} is called a Document.

A private app's card in **Apps** shows the name, sentence and icon the app gives itself.

An app your plan doesn't include leaves the rail and isn't offered in **Apps**.

A new workspace arrives set up in its country, time zone and currency, with its owner named.

A company the setup adds after the first, or that an administrator adds in {account_center}, can invoice and run payroll at once: it gets the financial year it is in, a holiday list, its leave and payroll periods, an account on every salary component and its own letter head.

The apps' agents are not listed in **People** and take no seat.

A new workspace also arrives with the apps, companies, working week and people its owner chose when signing up.

{setup_center}, {access_center}, {notification_center}, {shortcut_center}, Help and What's New open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above their sections.

A module's **Settings** has **Help** in its corner, opening the page that explains them.

{update_center} starts with **This Workspace**. **Next Update** says when {one} next updates your workspace, what the update brings, and whether it waits for your organisation to agree to new terms. **Update Window** is the day and hour updates run, which a Workspace Administrator changes there, and **Update Now** updates at once when a new release is waiting. They were under **Plan and Credits** in {account_center}.

Help and What's New list only the apps that are on, and **Ctrl+K** finds every Help page by its title. Help explains {update_center} under **Updates**.

{one_chat} has its place on the rail, after {one_mail}.
