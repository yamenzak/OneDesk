- **Home** is rebuilt around the workspace: what waits for your approval in
  every app, the announcements, what is new in your apps and, for an
  administrator, the workspace at a glance. What is asked of you, a file
  someone requested, is there too, and you send it from Home.
- **Dashboards** left One. Each app's are on its own rail, and you make and
  change them in OneStudio.
- **Announcements**, **Recycle Bin**, **Contacts** and **Addresses** are now
  the workspace's ordinary lists. Announcements opens on what is showing;
  Recycle Bin restores several at once from Actions.
- **Lists** has its own section in the left pane, each list with its icon.
- One's settings are split: Company, Branding, Region and Formats, Sign-in,
  Sharing, and **Pictures**, which draws a picture in the style you pick for
  each kind of record that has none.
- **NotificationCenter** and **AccessCenter** have a row per app, with Yours
  and Workspace, or Roles, People and On One Record, at the top of each.
- In every centre, Help and Enlarge are in the dialog's top corner.
- The portal's paragraph of the agreements moved to OnePortal, and the
  privacy notice says where Your Data is now.
