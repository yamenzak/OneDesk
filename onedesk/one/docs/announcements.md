# Announcements

**Announcements** are messages from administrators to everyone in the
workspace, such as an office closure or a new expense policy. The page shows
each in full, with who posted it and when.

- **Showing Now** lists the ones running along the top bar. **Earlier** keeps
  the ones that have stopped.
- To post one, click **New Announcement**, enter a title and write the
  message. Only administrators post announcements. A note anybody else
  writes stays private.

On the top bar:

- Every public announcement runs along the top bar until **Show Until** (a
  week after it was posted if left empty). The newest five run at once.
  Hover to stop it, and click one to read it all. **×** folds them to the
  megaphone with how many there are, for you only, and they stay folded
  until you click the megaphone or a new one is posted.
- New announcements are **Public** and **Show When People Sign In** by
  default. Untick **Show When People Sign In** for one that only runs on the
  bar.
- Each person sees it once, the next time they open One, until **Show Until**
  (a week if left empty).
- Tick **Every Time They Sign In** to show it at every sign-in.
- Everyone is also notified right away, in One and by email if they chose
  (**Announcement**).

For administrators, each announcement says how many people have seen it.
Click that to see who. **Edit** changes it, and **Stop Showing** takes it off
the top bar at once and moves it to Earlier. OneAI can answer **Who has not
read it?**
