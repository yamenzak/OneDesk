# What One Is

One is the workspace itself: the frame every app sits in, Home, the Address Book, Announcements and the Recycle Bin, and the centres that hold the workspace's settings, who may do what, and how people are told. Its rail is the first on the dock.

## Finding Your Way

One's rail has these groups:

- **Home**, **Announcements** and **Recycle Bin**, first.
- **Address Book**: the workspace's **Contacts** and **Addresses**. Customers,
  suppliers, companies and employees all use them, so they live here rather
  than in one app. You see the ones your work lets you open.
- **Centers**: AccountCenter, ControlCenter, NotificationCenter,
  AccessCenter, TrustCenter, AICenter, HelpCenter and UpdateCenter. Each
  opens over the page you are on. AccessCenter is for administrators.

Click a group's name to fold it away.

Each app's dashboards are on its own rail, as **Overview** rows.

A link to a page that doesn't exist shows **Page not found**, and a page you
may not open shows **No access**. **Go Back** and **Go Home** take you on.

## The Frame

Every screen sits inside one frame: the rail of apps down the left, a thin
bar across the top and one across the bottom. The screen itself is inset in
it, its corners rounded. The frame is dark when the theme is light, and a
shade lighter than the page when the theme is dark.

The top bar is the workspace and what reaches you. On the left is the
workspace's name. In the middle run the announcements. On the right are
**Search**, then **Reading** and **Notifications**.

The bottom bar is the screen you are on and what is running, like a taskbar.
On the left is what the screen says about itself: how many rows a list
shows and its paging, or how many files a folder holds. Then any workbook
you left for another screen, under OneWorkbook's mark. On the right are **Check In** and **Timers**,
then **OneAI**, and at the end today's date and the time, which open OneCalendar. A number on
an icon says how much waits there. **Check In** says *Check In* when your
shift has started and you haven't, and *Late* once you are late. On a phone
there are no bars, and their tools are at the top of the panel instead.

The frame's colour is the workspace's to choose: **Frame Colour** under
One's ControlCenter › Branding. Its writing turns dark on a light
colour, and in a dark theme the colour is deepened. Leave it empty for One's
own.

**Background Tasks** shows in the top bar only while a long job you started is
still running. Click it to see what is running.

## Moving Around

In every app the panel on the left lists the app's places, and stays open on
its lists and reports.

The names at the top of a page take you elsewhere. Tap the place's name,
**Projects** for example, to go to another place in the app. Tap a record's
name to open another record of its kind, the latest ones first. Type to
search them, as you would in a field that picks a record. **All Projects**
at the foot opens the whole list.

On a phone the top of a page is two bars. The first has the menu, the place,
the page's buttons and search. The second is for the record you have open:
its name, its status and a project's **All Work**. On a list the second bar
has **List View**.

A record's own page, such as a project's, reads like a form. Its views are
tabs across the top, which slide sideways when they don't all fit, and who it's
assigned to, its attachments, tags, sharing and likes are on the right. Its
**Activity** is where you write a comment or send an email, as on a form:
the comment box is at the top, **New Email** opens the email window, and
**Reply** or **Reply All** on an email answers it. The newest is first.

The round OneAI button in the corner opens OneAI on any page. It offers
suggestions for that page, and you can ask it how anything in One works.

## Searching

Click **Search** in the bar, or press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac). Type the name of
a list, a report or a page to go there, or a word to search records for it.
You only find what you can open. The records of an app the workspace turned
off are left out.

## Your Menu

Click your picture at the bottom of the rail for your menu:

- **Settings** opens your Profile.
- **Theme** chooses **Light**, **Dark** or **Automatic**, which follows your
  computer's setting.
- **Reload** reloads One, for when a page looks out of date.
- **Logout** signs you out.

The space's name at the top of the panel is a title, not a menu.

## Asking OneAI

The OneAI button at the bottom of every page opens the OneAI panel. Every
conversation is saved, and the arrow at the top left lists them.

- **Model**: One picks the model that answers, by the kind of work. There is
  nothing to choose in the panel.
- **Records read**: the answer comes first. Below it are the records OneAI
  read, with one record as a card and several as a list. The list shows three.
  **Show more** shows the rest, and **Open List** opens them in their own list.
- **Name**: after its first answer, OneAI names the conversation in a few
  words. Click the name at the top to change it. OneAI won't rename it after
  that.
- **Tools**: OneAI uses the tools that fit your question and the page you're
  on, and adds others, such as mail or workspace settings, when it needs them.
  You don't choose them.

Ask OneAI how anything in One works, in your own words and language. It
answers from each app's Help, and says which section the answer comes
from. If they don't cover something, it says so instead of guessing.
