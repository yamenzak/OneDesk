# Home

**Home** is the first page you see in One. It shows what's waiting for you
today, across every app you use. Each number counts only records you can
open, and an app the workspace turned off is left out. Click a number to open
its list.

- **Tasks Due or Late**: tasks assigned to you that are due today or overdue,
  from OneTask's My Tasks.
- **Meetings Today**: events on your own calendar today.
- **Documents Waiting**: documents OneAI read that are waiting for you.
- **OneAI Suggestions**: suggestions you haven't approved or refused yet.
- **Approvals Waiting**: leave applications and expense claims waiting for
  your approval.

Under the numbers, **Your Tasks** lists the first tasks due today or late,
and **Today** what is on your calendar, each opening the task or the event.
**Waiting for Your Approval** lists the requests waiting on you, oldest
first. **New Task** and **New Event** are at the top of the page.

Administrators also see **The Workspace Needs You** when something needs
attention:

- A mailbox isn't connecting.
- A domain isn't working.
- OneAI credits are running low.
- Storage or the database is nearly full.
- Every seat is taken.
- A payment is overdue.
- The holiday list ends with no list after it.

Each one opens the page where you fix it.

OneAI offers **What needs me today?** here.
