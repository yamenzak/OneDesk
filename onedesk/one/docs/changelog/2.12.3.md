In OneCloud, an Excel, CSV or Word file opens in OneWorkbook or OneWriter when they keep everything in it, and as an exact preview when they don't, so looking at a file never changes it. **Edit**, at the top of the preview and in the file's right-click menu, offers **Edit in OneWorkbook** or **Edit in OneWriter**, **Edit in OneOS** with what an hour of it costs, and **Download**. When the file has something the editor can't keep, such as comments or charts, you are told which and choose **Edit in OneOS** or **Edit Here Anyway**. A PowerPoint file offers **Make a Presentation**, a new OneSlide presentation from it, and a file with macros or an older .xls offers **Make a One Copy**.

**Edit in OneOS** opens the file in a full office app in a new tab, and each save is kept as a new version of the file. Only one person edits a file in OneOS at a time: the file's row says who, as it says who is editing it in OneWorkbook or OneWriter, and anyone else can **Make a Copy** or **View Read-Only**. Each minute OneOS is open uses OneCredits, and it closes by itself after 15 minutes without use or after 8 hours.
