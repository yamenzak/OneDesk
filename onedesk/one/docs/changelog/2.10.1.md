OneHR's leave, attendance, claims, payslips, hiring and reviews say what matters at the top of each record, as the other apps' records do.
- A record whose company is not asked for goes to the workspace's default company, and follows it when the default changes.
- Choosing a record in a field offers every one you may pick, even one you may not open.
- OneFactory opens on a Home of its own.
- OneQuality opens on a Home of its own.
- With more than one company, departments, leave and payroll periods, pricing and tax rules, POS profiles, vehicles and about twenty more show which company each belongs to, and filter by it.
