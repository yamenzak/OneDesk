News comes to One. An administrator adds News Sources, each a feed, Google News, OneAI or written by hand, and each fetches on its own schedule and keeps only its newest items. A library of ready feeds is one search away. The top bar switches between announcements and news, and each person chooses the sources they follow. The terms, the privacy policy and the subprocessors now say who owns what a source shows, that Google News is for a preview only, and that Google LLC is reached when it is on.
