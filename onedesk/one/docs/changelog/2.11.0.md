OneAI credits are now called **OneCredits**, since they pay for more than OneAI: reading files, converting them, and mail One sends for you. Your balance and what it buys are unchanged, and the Terms of Service say OneCredits too.

Credits you buy now last 12 months, and administrators can set a **Monthly Limit** under the OneCredits tile in **Plan and Credits**: once it is reached, nothing more is charged until the next month. A workspace behind on payment is charged nothing new until it is paid. The Terms of Service say so.
