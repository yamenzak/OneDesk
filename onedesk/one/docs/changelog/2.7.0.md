Google News sources no longer reach Google from the workspace. One's account fetches each topic or search for every workspace that follows it, one at a time and a few times a day, and pauses when Google refuses, so how often a Google News source fetches is no longer set on it. The privacy policy and the Google News service now say the account alone asks Google.
