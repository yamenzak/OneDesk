- **Moving Around** in Help says how **Apps** and the **☰** button work on a phone.
- **Several companies.** In a workspace with more than one company, the
  company you work in is beside the workspace's name in the top bar, and at
  the top of the **☰** menu on a phone: click it to change to another, and
  every Home, figure and new record follows. Records of each app made ready
  for several companies show their **Company**, OneInventory first.
- A link to a screen your role doesn't open, such as **Reorder** for
  someone in sales, says **No access** instead of **Page not found**.
- Screens that update as records change open with less waiting.
- With more than one company, every list of a record that has one opens on
  the company you work in, and its **Filter** says so.
