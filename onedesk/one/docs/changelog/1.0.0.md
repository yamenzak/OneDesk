One has a Help of its own now. It explains the sidebar, Home, the Address
Book, announcements, records, the Recycle Bin, notifications, access and the
audit log, your data, the portal and Settings.

- A role can be given Everyone's Deletions in AccessCenter. Whoever holds it
  sees and puts back what anybody deleted, of the kinds of record they may
  read. One Manager has it to start with.
- The Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, Privacy Policy, Data
  Processing Addendum, Subprocessors and AI Addendum say the same things in
  shorter sentences.
