An email of a notification you chose now ends with **Turn off these emails**. It turns off email for that one notification, as its switch in **NotificationCenter** does, without signing in, and the page it opens has **Turn On Again**. Security notices and other emails that are always mailed have no such link.

A new workspace starts in the time zone of whoever signed up for it.
