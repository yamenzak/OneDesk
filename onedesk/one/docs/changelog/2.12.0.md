Workspaces are checked with less work behind the scenes.

Administrators can turn on **Training Copies** under **Optional Services**, and each workspace now sends One's team a short summary of its errors every hour.

Your database and settings are now backed up every four hours, and your files every night. The Terms and the data processing agreement say so.
