# Keyboard Shortcuts

## ShortcutCenter

**ShortcutCenter** lists every key {one} answers to, app by app, and where each
one works: **Anywhere**, **In a List**, **In a Record**, **In a Calendar**, or
in one app's own screen, like {one_mail}. A key made for the screen you're on
comes before one that works anywhere.

Open it from **Centers** on {one}'s rail, from **Keyboard Shortcuts** at the end
of an app's Settings, or press **Shift+/**.

## Change a Shortcut

1. Click **Change** beside a shortcut.
2. Press the keys you want, or click them on the keyboard picture. Click
   **Ctrl**, **Alt** or **Shift** on the picture to hold it, then click a key.
3. The side buttons of the mouse work too. Click one on the mouse picture, or
   press it.

**No Shortcut** leaves it without a key. **Esc** or **Cancel** stops
listening and keeps what was there.

If another shortcut already uses those keys where this one works, One asks
first. **Use It Here** moves the keys to this shortcut, and the other one is
left without them.

Keys your browser keeps for itself, like **Ctrl+W** and **Ctrl+T**, can't be
used.

## Yours Alone

What you change is yours. Nobody else's keys move. **Reset** beside a
shortcut puts its keys back, and **Reset All** puts every one back.

## Keys on the Picture

Hover over a shortcut and its keys light up on the keyboard. A dot on a key
means some shortcut uses it. Click a key to see what it does.
