# Weather

One shows the weather for the places you choose: on {one_display}'s screens,
and beside the clock. It comes from OpenWeather, through {one}'s account.

- **Weather is off** until an administrator turns it on in **{trust_center}**.
  While it is off, weather widgets show nothing and no place can be found.
- Add a place under {one} › **Weather Places**. Type a town or city in
  **Place** and pick it from the list, or click the location button in the
  field to use the town you are in. Its region, country and coordinates are
  filled in for you. Choose **Metric** or **Imperial** for each place.
- Tick **Default Place** on the one to show beside the clock, at the end of
  the bottom bar. Point at it for the day's high and low; click it to open
  the place. Only one place is the default.
- A weather source in {one_display} names one of these places. Every screen and
  widget that shows the same place shares its weather.
- The next hours are fetched again every two hours, and the next days twice
  a day. "Now" is the hour that covers the present.
- **The first place shown each day is free.** Each other place costs five
  credits for each day it is shown. A place nobody shows that day costs
  nothing.

Only a place's coordinates, and the name you search for when you add one,
are sent. Nothing about a person is.
