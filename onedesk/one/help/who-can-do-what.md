# Who Can Do What

Everybody uses Home, the Address Book, Announcements and the Recycle Bin, each showing only what their work lets them open, and what
they deleted themselves. Posting an announcement, and the
workspace's settings, who may do what and the workspace's own
notifications are a Workspace Administrator's, who can do everything.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Read the announcements, use Home and the Address Book, and keep their own notifications. |
| **Manager** | People you choose, under People in {access_center} | Also see and put back what anybody deleted. |
