# Home

**Home** is the first page you see in {one}. It shows what only {one} sees
across your apps. Your tasks, meetings and documents are on their own apps'
homes.

- **Waiting for Your Approval**: leave applications, expense claims and
  approval steps waiting on you, from every app, oldest first.
- **Asked of You**: files someone has asked you for, from any app, such as
  HR asking for your passport. Click one to send it, with anything asked
  beside it.
- **Announcements**: what the office is saying now. **All Announcements**
  opens every one.
- **New in Your Apps**: apps with a version you haven't opened yet. Click
  one to see what changed.

Administrators also see **The Workspace**: its people against its seats,
the apps turned on, storage used and {one_credits} left. Each opens where
it is managed.

And **The Workspace Needs You** when something needs attention:

- A mailbox isn't connecting.
- A domain isn't working.
- {one_credits} are running low.
- Storage or the database is nearly full.
- Every seat is taken.
- A payment is overdue.
- The holiday list ends with no list after it.

Each one opens the page where you fix it.

{one_ai} offers **What needs me today?** here.
