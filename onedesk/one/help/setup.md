# Setting Up the Apps

**{setup_center}** lists what each app needs set up so it works the way your
company does, and which of it is done. Open it from **Centers** on {one}'s
rail, from **Getting Started** at the top of an app's Settings while a step
waits, or search for it.

## Overview

**Overview** lists every app that has steps, with how many are done. An app
with nothing left says **Done**. Apps turned off in {one_marketplace} aren't listed.

## An App's Steps

Each app's tab lists its steps, in order.

| You see | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set Up** | Opens the step. A step asked when the workspace was set up shows the same fields here, and **Save** keeps them. Any other step opens the tab in the app's Settings that does it. |
| **Mark as Done** | Takes a step off the list without changing anything. |
| **Done** | The step is finished. |

A step left for later when the workspace was set up waits here until
somebody does it.

> **You see the steps your role can do.** An administrator sees every app's.
> Someone who manages one app sees that app's steps alone.

## After Turning an App On

**Install** on an app's page in {one_marketplace} opens {setup_center} on
that app's steps, when it has any.

## Order of Apps

**Order of Apps**, under **Workspace**, is for administrators. Drag the apps
into the order the rail shows them in and press **Save**. It is the order for
everybody who hasn't arranged their own with **Arrange Apps**. **Reset** goes
back to {one}'s own order.
