# Announcements

**Announcements** are messages from administrators to everyone in the
workspace, such as an office closure or a new expense policy. One ›
**Announcements** lists them, newest first.

- The list opens on **Status: Showing**, the ones running along the top bar.
  Choose **Ended** in the Status filter for the ones that have stopped.
- Click one to read it in full. Administrators change it there with **Edit
  mode**, and see who has read it under **Seen By**: everybody who opened it
  from the top bar.
- To post one, click **Add Note** here or **New Announcement** on Home, give
  it a title, write the message, and
  set **Show Until** if it should run for more or less than a week. Only
  administrators post announcements. A note anybody else writes stays
  private.

On the top bar:

- Every announcement runs along the top bar until **Show Until**, a week
  after it was posted if left empty. The newest five run at once. Hover to
  stop it, and click one to read it all. **×** folds them to the megaphone
  with how many there are, for you only, and they stay folded until you
  click the megaphone or a new one is posted.
- Everyone is also notified right away, in {one} and by email if they chose
  (**Announcement**).

**Stop Showing**, on an announcement or under **Actions** in the list for
the ones ticked, takes it off the top bar at once and marks it Ended. {one_ai}
can answer **Who has not read it?**
