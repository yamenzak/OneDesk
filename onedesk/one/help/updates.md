# Updates

## {update_center}

**{update_center}** says when {one} next updates your workspace, and what each
app changed in every version. Open it from **Centers** on {one}'s rail, or
from the version beside an app's name at the foot of its rail.

## Next Update

**Next Update** shows the day and time of the next update, once a new release
is waiting for your workspace. The workspace is offline for a few minutes
while it updates. In the three days before, a note on everybody's bar says so.

**What It Brings** lists the versions the update brings, by app. Open one to
read what changes.

**Update Now**, for a Workspace Administrator, updates the workspace at once
instead of waiting for the window. When no new release is waiting, {one}
says so and nothing happens.

> **An update waits for your organisation's agreement.** When {one}'s terms
> change and your organisation hasn't agreed yet, **Next Update** says so and
> the update waits, without **Update Now**. It goes ahead 30 days after its
> window even so.

## Update Window

**Update Window** is the day and hour updates run, in the workspace's time
zone. It starts as Saturday at 01:00. A Workspace Administrator changes the
**Day** and **Hour** and presses **Save**. Unless somebody presses **Update
Now**, a new release waits at least three days before it runs.

## What's New

Each app has its own branch under **What's New**, newest version first. A dot
marks a version you haven't read. A version marked **Coming** is in the next
update. **Read More** under a version opens the Help pages it changed.
