Present from a meeting room's computer with your phone as the remote, and nobody signs in.

- Open the presentation's link on the room's computer and click Phone
  Remote: the slides fill the screen and show a code. Scan it and your
  phone moves the slides and points at them.
- The first phone to open a code keeps it; anybody else who scans it is
  told another phone is already the remote. This holds in the presenter
  view too, which now has Use Another Phone.
- Signed in on your phone, you see your notes there as well.
- The remote ends when the page closes or the link is turned off.
