Words on a slide can come from your records and stay up to date.

- **File › Data from Records** adds the record a presentation is filed on,
  any other record, one linked from it, or a list, and puts a field on the
  slide. It is filled each time the slide is shown, on a screen and in
  PowerPoint too, and shows each person only what they may read.
- A list goes in as a table, or as its count or a total.
- **Freeze Data** writes in what the fields say now, keeping what they were
  in Version History, and **Make One for Each…** files a copy on each record
  you pick.
