OneSlide is called **OneDeck** from now on. Your presentations, templates,
fonts and shared links are where they were and work as before; only the name
on the screen, in Help and in the agreements is new. Its roles are now
**OneDeck User** and **OneDeck Manager**, held by the same people.

Help names the other editors by their new names too, OneDoc and OneSheet.
