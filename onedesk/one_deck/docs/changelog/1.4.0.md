Speaker notes, a presenter view, and a PDF with notes.

- **Speaker Notes**, under the slide, keeps what you will say over it. Only
  you see them.
- **Present › Presenter View** shows your notes, the next slide and the time
  here and plays the slides in a window of their own for the projector. The
  arrow keys move both together, with no network needed between them.
- **File › Export as PDF with Notes** prints each slide on an A4 page above
  its notes, as words you can find and copy.
