OneSlide's editor now also lays out OneDisplay's screens.

- A widget collection opens here from **Edit Layout**, at the screen's own
  shape, standing or on its side.
- Its widgets are on the slide with everything else: name, stack, lock and
  undo them in **Layers**, and set them up in the panel.
