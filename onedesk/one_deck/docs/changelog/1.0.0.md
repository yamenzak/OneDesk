OneSlide makes presentations, kept in OneCloud beside the rest of your files.
This is the version it is released at.

- **New › Presentation** in OneCloud makes one, in a light or dark theme or
  from a template your business keeps.
- Text, pictures, videos, shapes, lines and tables, with fades, blur and
  CSS for anything the panel doesn't do, and Layers to name, hide, lock and
  restack what is on a slide.
- Version History keeps what a presentation was, and goes back to it.
- Export as PDF, or play it full screen with **Present**.
- OneAI designs a slide, or makes a picture or a short video, from a
  sentence. This adds a part to the AI Addendum.
- A OneDisplay playlist plays presentations on your screens.
- AccessCenter decides who may keep company templates.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneSlide Is.
