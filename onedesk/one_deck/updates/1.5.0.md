Rulers and guides to line things up by eye.

- **File › Show Rulers and Guides** puts a ruler along the top and the side
  of the slide. Drag out of one to lay a guide; anything moved or resized
  near it snaps to it. Drag a guide off the slide to remove it.
- **Add Margin Guides** lays four, 40 pixels in from each edge, and
  **Clear Guides** takes them all away. Guides are kept with the
  presentation and never show when it is presented or printed.
- The slide now fits between the slides on the left and the panel on the
  right, so its edges are never hidden under either.
