OneSlide stands on its own.

- **Design a Slide**, **Make a Picture** and **Make a Video** are OneSlide's
  own OneAI jobs now, written for a presentation rather than a sign, and
  listed under OneSlide in AICenter.
- A video on a slide remembers how long it is, so wherever the presentation
  plays by itself it waits for the video to finish before moving on.
- The Help speaks of presentations only; how a presentation plays on a TV is
  in OneDisplay's Help.
