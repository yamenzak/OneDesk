Arabic fonts, your own fonts, and words that run the right way.

- **Family** now offers seven Arabic fonts, each shown in its own letters,
  beside the rest. Screens and PDFs draw them too.
- A manager adds the company's own font with **Add a Font…**, from a file,
  or from Google's catalogue once **Fonts from Google** is on in
  TrustCenter. Fonts are kept in the workspace, in OneCloud › Fonts.
- Each paragraph runs the way its first letter does, so Arabic and English
  sit in one box. **Direction** sets a box to one way.
- In a workspace that reads Arabic, a new presentation starts in an Arabic
  font.
- The privacy policy and the subprocessors list now say what Fonts from
  Google sends, and to whom.
