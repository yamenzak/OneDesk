A project template opens on its form, like every other record. At the top are its **Tasks**, its **Duration** and the **Projects** started from it, with **New Project** beside its name. Its tasks are drawn under them and change in place as before, and its terms are under the tasks.

**Draft a template** is offered on a template as well as on the list.
