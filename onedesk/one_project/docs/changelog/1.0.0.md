OneProject runs work bigger than a task in One: its tasks, its time, what it
costs and what the customer is billed. This is the version it is released at.

- A project is Fixed Price, Time and Materials, a Retainer or Internal, and
  its page is laid out for its kind: the figures that matter, and what needs
  someone now.
- Its tasks show as a table, a board or a timeline, and are the same tasks
  people see on My Tasks.
- Bill Progress, Invoice Time and Invoice Month make the customer's
  invoices, and subcontracts, materials and a budget by cost type keep what
  it costs in sight.
- Work grows into sub-projects, and extra work a customer asks for becomes
  one once they order it.
- The privacy notice now says plainly what the team's answers to Ask for
  Updates and days on site keep, one point to a sentence.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneProject Is.
