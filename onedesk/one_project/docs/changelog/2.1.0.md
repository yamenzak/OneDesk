- A project's **Tasks** tab lists each task under its milestone, the
  milestone in bold and its steps under it. **Milestone** in the sort puts
  them back that way.
- A long task name wraps instead of being cut.
- With more than one company, a project and a timesheet show their
  **Company**, and their lists open on the company you work in.
- A project you may read but not change opens without a message saying you
  have no access.
- A cancelled project no longer offers a contract or an invoice.
- A project opens faster: each tab's figures load when you open the tab.
- A timesheet says what it is worth at the top, and its list counts them by
  where they stand.
- The project's tour points at its four figures.
- Help has pictures of a project's page, its Tasks, Costing and Team tabs,
  and Workload.
- A task with no end date no longer counts as late on a project, in its figures or when OneAI is asked.
