- On a project's **Tasks** tab, clicking a task shows it in a panel beside
  the project, with **Open in full page** to change it.
- Tasks done or cancelled are hidden until you press **Completed**.
- Sorted by **Status**, **Priority** or **End Date**, the tasks are grouped
  under a heading for each, with how many it holds.
- The customer and the sales order are at the top of the project's panel.
