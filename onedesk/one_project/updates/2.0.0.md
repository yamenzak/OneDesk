A project opens on its form, like every other record:

- At the top are **% Completed**, **Cost**, **Billed** and **Margin So Far**, with the kind's main button beside the name: **Make Order**, **Add Contract**, **Bill Progress**, **Invoice Time**, **Invoice Month** or **Post Update**.
- **Overview** shows how the work is going, then what a project of its kind is run by: billing, orders, quotations, the budget, milestones, months, subcontracts, retention, contracts, **Needs You**, updates and sub-projects.
- **Tasks** lists its tasks, changed in place, with **Board** and **Schedule** at the top.
- **Timesheet** and **Suppliers** are tabs of their own, the money and the customer's invoices start **Costing**, and the team is on **More Info**.
- **Activity** on the right has the documents made for the project, the tasks added and completed and the hours logged each week, beside its changes and comments.
- Completing or cancelling a project with open work asks first, and mentioning somebody who can't see the project asks whether to add them.
