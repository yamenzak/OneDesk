# Settings

**{one_project} Settings**, first under the arrow at the right of the top bar, opens {one_project}'s settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | {notification_center}, on {one_project}'s part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | {access_center}, for an administrator: who sees every project. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **{one_ai}** | {ai_center}, on what {one_ai} may do in projects. See [Asking {one_ai}](asking-oneai). |
| **Your Data** | {trust_center}, on {one_project}'s part of the agreements. |

## Projects Settings

**Projects Settings**, in the ··· menu on **Project**, is for whoever may change it. It
opens beside {one_project}'s rail, as Rates and Project Types do. It holds:

- **Allow Overlapping Time per User** and **Allow Overlapping Time per
  Employee**: on, one person may log two timesheet rows for the same hours,
  such as a supervisor on two sites at once. Off, the second is refused.
- **Bring Timesheets into Sales Invoices**: on, choosing a project on a sales
  invoice adds its billable timesheets. Invoice Time on a project does
  this either way.
- **Hold Retention**, **Count Time By** for new projects, and **Activity for
  Days on Site**.

A day on site costs what its activity costs an hour, so give **Site Work** a
rate under **Activity Type**. With none, a day on site costs nothing.

## Rates

Each activity, such as Design or Execution, has a default rate an hour costs
and a rate it's billed at. Under **Rates for a Person**, give somebody their
own rates for that activity. A timesheet uses the person's rate when there's
one, and the activity's when there isn't.
