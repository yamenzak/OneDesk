# What OneProject Is

OneProject is where work bigger than a task is run: a villa's windows, a
website, an office move. A project holds its tasks, its time, what it costs
and what it's billed. A project's quotation, sales order, timesheets and
invoices are the same records the rest of One uses.

**OneTask and OneProject share the same tasks.** OneTask is what each person
has to do. OneProject is how a project is going, for the people who run it. A
project task assigned to you shows on your My Tasks and on the project's board.

## Finding Your Way

**OneProject** in the dock opens it. The panel on the left lists its four
places, and its reports below them.

- **Project**: every project you can see. Employee onboarding and exit
  checklists belong to OneHR and aren't listed.
- **Workload**: how many hours of open work each person has in a week.
- **Timesheet**: the time people have logged, across projects, newest first. Each
  timesheet shows who, the project, the days it covers, its hours and how
  many of them are billable. Filter it by project, status, start date or
  person. Its days are one column, the first and last only when they
  differ, and hours read as hours. A timesheet opens on what is left to
  bill on it, in orange, or that none of it is billable, in gray, over a
  track from Logged to Billed and what its billable hours are worth at the
  project's rates. A timesheet prints as **One
  Timesheet**, for a customer billed for time to sign.
- **Project Template**: the tasks a kind of job usually has, to start a project
  from (see [Starting a Project](starting-a-project)).
- **Reports**, across all projects:
  - **Project Progress**: how far along each project is, with its tasks,
    how many are done and how many are late. It opens on open projects;
    filter it by status or priority. Onboarding and exit checklists are
    left out.
  - **Project Profitability**: what billed timesheets earned against what
    the people who logged them cost. It counts only people paid from their
    timesheets.
  - **Timesheet Billing Summary**: hours worked and hours billed, by person or by
    project.
  - **Daily Timesheet Summary**: each timesheet line in a range of days, opening on
    today.
  - **Stock by Project**: what each project's items cost, bought, issued
    from stock and delivered, beside the project's value.

  A report shows only to people whose role may open it.

On a phone, tap the place's name at the top, **Project**, to go to another.
**Activity Type**, what an hour of each
activity costs and is billed at, is in the ··· menu on **Project**, and so
is **Project Update**, the progress notes a project's people send when its
Collect Progress asks them.

On a project, its name at the top opens your other open projects and
**All Projects**.

Tasks are in OneTask, and a project's own tasks are on its **Tasks** tab. Project
types are in OneProject's **Lists**, the last row of its rail.
