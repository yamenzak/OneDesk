# Who Can Do What

Anybody may be on a project. Seeing every project in the workspace, not only
the ones you're on, is for fewer people.

## Who Runs a Project

**Project Manager** is the one person who runs a project. It starts as
whoever created it. The manager always sees the project, whether or not
they're a member. Filter **Project** by it, or by **Health**.

## Who Sees a Project

**A project's members** are the people listed under **Members** on its **More Info** tab.
They see the project and every task in it. Nobody else opens it, whether they
work in projects, the stores or buying: Projects Managers and accounts see
every project, and whoever created a project sees it.

**A project with no members is open** to everyone who works in projects, so a
small team never has to add anyone.

**A task's own people always see it.** Whoever is assigned a task sees it on
My Tasks, member or not, but nothing else of the project.

Anybody who puts a project on a quotation, an order, a receipt or an invoice
picks from every project in that field. Picking one doesn't open it.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Work on the projects they're on, and on open ones. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also see every project in the workspace. |

Whoever holds **Projects Manager** is also given {one_project}'s Manager role.
Money on a project shows to whoever may see the documents behind it: its
orders, invoices and bills.
