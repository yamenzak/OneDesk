# Your Account

## Your Profile

**Profile** holds your name, photo, mobile, bio, language and time zone. **Sign Out**, beside your photo, signs you out on this device.
Change any of them and press **Save**. If you are an employee, three more
parts sit under it, each saved on its own:

- **Personal Details**: where you live, which email HR writes to
  (**Preferred Contact Email**), who to call in an emergency, and what only
  you and HR see.
- **Work**: your job as HR keeps it, and your education and past work.
- **Documents**: your ID, passport and permits.

HR keeps your job and bank details, and hears when you change where you
live, who to call, your education, your past work or your documents.

**Documents** lists your ID, passport and permits. Add a new one, or
change one when you renew it, with its number, dates and a scan. The kinds
you can pick are your country's and the ones every country has. One that has
run out is named above the list. When {one_ai} reads a renewed document sent in
by mail or uploaded, it updates your record the same way.

**Your Files**, under the list, is your folder in {one_cloud}: the scan on each
document lands there when you save, and you can add any other paper HR
should keep. HR sees the same folder on your employee record. Taking a file
out again is HR's.

Your name, gender, birth date, mobile, photo and bio are the same on your
profile and your employee record: a change to one is a change to both,
whether you make it or HR does.

**Write My Bio**, beside Bio, asks you to say a little about yourself. {one_ai}
writes a short bio from that and your employee record, and **Use** puts it in
the field. Change it as you like, then press **Save**. Nothing is saved until
you do.

If somebody else saved your profile after you opened it, your save stops
and says so, so nothing they wrote is lost. Open it again and make your
change.

**Your Data** at the end of your profile asks for a copy of everything {one}
keeps about you, or for your account to be deleted.

## How You Sign In

**Sign-in** starts with anything weak, said plainly: someone getting your
password wrong, or a password never changed with no passkey beside it. Then a
tile for each way in:

- **Password**: when it last changed, and **Change Password**.
- **Two-Factor**: whether the workspace asks for a code after your password,
  and how. The first time, you get an email to set up your authenticator
  app; it lists the workspace by its name. On a new phone, press **New
  Phone** and set it up again the next time you sign in. An administrator
  sees **Turn On for the Workspace** while it is off.
- **Passkey**: signs you in with your face, fingerprint or screen lock
  instead of a password. Press **Register This Device** on the computer or
  phone you use.

Below that is every place you are signed in. **Sign Out** ends one, and
**Sign Out Other Sessions** ends all but this one. You hear about every
sign-in from a device you have not used before.
