# Companies

**Companies** lists the workspace's companies, the default one first. Open
one to change its details, its accounts and its defaults.

## Adding a Company

Press **Add Company** and give:

| Field | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Company Name** | The name on its invoices, payslips and reports. |
| **Country** | Its holidays, its usual days off and its currency. |
| **Currency** | Its own currency. Every figure of the company is kept in it. |
| **Chart of Accounts** | **Standard**, or **Standard with Numbers** to number each account. |
| **Letter Head** | **New Letter Head**, drawn with the company's name, or the default company's. |

The company is ready to invoice and to run payroll at once: it gets its
accounts, the financial year it is in, a holiday list with its country's
public holidays, its leave and payroll periods, and an account on every
salary component.

> Selling to it from a price list in another currency uses the exchange
> rate between the two. Add the rate in {one_book} if your accountant uses
> their own.

Once there is a second company, records that belong to one company ask
which, and every Home and figure shows one company at a time, in its own
currency.
