# People and Devices

## Inviting Somebody

Open **People** and press **Invite People**. Give their address and name,
and choose what they may use in each app. Everybody has {one}, {one_cloud},
{one_mail}, {one_task} and {one_calendar}; the other apps are given here. They get a
mail with a link to set their password, which lasts a week.

The list shows each person's apps in one line, such as *Manager in {one_crm},
{one_book} +3*, and how they sign in: **Passkey**, **Two-Factor**, **Two-Factor
Not Set Up** or **Password Only**.

## One Person

Open somebody in People to change what they may use, make them an
administrator, see where they are signed in and their last sign-ins, and
the records they are held to. **Actions** signs them out everywhere, resets
their two-factor when they lose their phone (they set it up again the next
time they sign in, and are told), sends them a password reset, sends a new invitation if they have not joined, or
turns them off. A person turned off can't sign in, and everything they made
stays.

What each role of an app may do is set in AccessCenter.

## The Company's Devices

**Devices** lists the company's computers and phones: the ones named and
marked as used by one person or shared. Under them, **Other Browsers** lists
every browser somebody signed in on that nobody has named yet; open one to
make it a company device. An unnamed browser with no passkey is forgotten
after a month unseen.

Open a device to rename it or change who uses it. At the foot, **Lost** signs
everyone out on it and turns off its passkeys at once; **Retire** takes it
off the list when it is no longer used.
