# Who Can Do What

Everybody keeps their own profile and sign-in. Everything about the
workspace is a Workspace Administrator's, who can do everything.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Keep their own profile and sign-in. |
| **Manager** | People you choose, under People in {access_center} | Also read Plan and Credits and the invoices. |

## What Stays With Administrators

Inviting and changing people, the company's devices, domains, changing the
plan and paying are always a Workspace Administrator's. Closing the
workspace is the person it is billed to.
