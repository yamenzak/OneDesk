AccountCenter's two roles take its name: **AccountCenter User**, which everyone holds, and **AccountCenter Manager**, which sees **Plan and Credits**. Whoever held them before holds them still, and you sign in, and your passkeys work, as before.

A private app's card in **Apps** shows the name, sentence and icon the app gives itself in its manifest, in your language where the app translates it.

Apps and services your plan doesn't include are no longer shown: your workspace learns from your account what its plan includes, such as **Training Copies**, and shows only what it can use.
