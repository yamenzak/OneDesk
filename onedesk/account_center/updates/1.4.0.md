If you administer the workspace, **Training Copies** under Workspace makes a temporary copy for people to train on: **Fresh**, set up like this workspace with no records, **From the Demo**, opened on the company of the field you choose, or **From Our Data**, last night's backup with each person's password cleared. It needs a plan that includes Training Copies, turned on in Optional Services. A copy says **Ready** when **Open** can sign you in, and **End** deletes it.

Booking One's bill in your books works in a workspace with a group of companies.

**Domains** and **Training Copies** now read in Arabic and German.
