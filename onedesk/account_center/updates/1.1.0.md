- If you are an employee, your profile has three more sections:
  **Personal Details**, **Work**, with your education and past work, and
  **Documents**, where you add and renew your ID, passport and permits. HR
  is told when you change any of them.
- **Sign-in** lets you reset your own two-factor sign-in for a new phone,
  and an administrator can reset somebody's from their page in People.
- **Devices** lists the company's devices first, then other browsers. A
  browser nobody named is forgotten after a month unseen.
- **Plan and Credits** shows seats, storage, database and credits side by
  side, each with its own button to buy more.
- A company device can be marked **Lost** or **Retired** from its page.
- The privacy notice says all of this, so everybody agrees to it once more.
