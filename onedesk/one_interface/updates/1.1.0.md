Every record and list is drawn in the Canvas look. A record opens with its picture, name, state and number, then its steps and up to four figures its app chooses, then its tabs; the panel holds its shortcuts and its activity as one thread, where your comments carry your picture and changes are small dots you can open. Fields nobody has filled wait behind Show Empty Fields, and a list opens with its states across the top, each one click away.
