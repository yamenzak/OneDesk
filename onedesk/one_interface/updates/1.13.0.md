The arrow at the right of the top bar lists every centre you may open, the screen's own first, then {one_hub}; on a phone it opens as a sheet. Every centre has a way up to {one_hub} above its name. The foot of each app's rail has **Help**, the tour and the version, the same in every app, and so does the end of the rail on a phone.

Every centre now opens the same way, from Settings to Help:

- A **Search** box above the sections finds a section by its name and what it is for, and in Help, What's New and {trust_center} by the words on its pages.
- A section's buttons, such as **Invite People**, sit on their own line under its title, never under the window's Help, Enlarge and Close.
- A table in a centre always fits the window. When a column has no room, it moves under the first column as a grey line, so nothing is cut off and nothing scrolls sideways, on a phone too. Its headings and columns stay lined up.

A folder you make in **Arrange Apps** stands on the rail where its first app is, and clicking it lists its apps beside the rail. **Apps**, at the bottom of the rail and in a phone's tab bar, lists your apps with the ones you opened last first, and finds apps and their pages as you type.

In Arabic, Help, What's New and the agreements, which are written in English, read left to right inside the right-to-left screen.
