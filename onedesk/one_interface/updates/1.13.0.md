Every centre now opens the same way, from Settings to Help:

- A **Search** box above the sections finds a section by its name and what it is for, and in Help, What's New and {trust_center} by the words on its pages.
- A section's buttons, such as **Invite People**, sit on their own line under its title, never under the window's Help, Enlarge and Close.
- A table in a centre always fits the window. When a column has no room, it moves under the first column as a grey line, so nothing is cut off and nothing scrolls sideways, on a phone too.
