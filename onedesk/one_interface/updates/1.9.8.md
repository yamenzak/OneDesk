On a phone:

- A section that folds (such as **Auto re-order**) opens and closes when you tap its heading.
- A tab with nothing filled in stays out of the way until you choose **Show Empty Fields**.
- Tap a field's **i** to read its help under it.
- **Back to top** shows after half a screen of scrolling.
- Long names in a list wrap instead of ending in "…".
