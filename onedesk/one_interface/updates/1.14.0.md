Your workspace can wear its own name, logo and colour. An administrator sets them once in **Branding** in {one}'s settings: the **Workspace Name** and **Workspace Logo** show at the start of the top bar, on the sign-in page, in the browser tab and at the top of mails, and the **Brand Colour** is the colour of the main buttons and of what is chosen on every screen, made lighter in dark so it can still be read. Left empty, each is {one}'s own.
