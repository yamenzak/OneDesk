On a phone, the tabs along the bottom now include **Apps**. It opens every
app and its places from any page. Type in its search to find a place in any
app, such as "leave" or "bank". **Documents**, **Check In**, **Timers** and
**OneAI** are at its top, and a dot on **Apps** says something waits there.
