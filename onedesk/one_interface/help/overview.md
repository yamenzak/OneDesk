# How Screens Are Laid Out

Every screen in {one} sits in the same window, so what you learn on one you
already know on the next. A list of invoices, an employee, a report and
{one_cloud} all keep the same parts in the same places.

## The Bar at the Top

**Back** and **Forward** move through what you opened, as in a browser.
**Up** goes to the list a record belongs to. The address in the middle says
where you are; click it and type to search, or press Ctrl+K from anywhere.
**Refresh** reloads what is on screen.

## The Actions

Under the address sit the things you can do here. The main one is dark and
comes first; the others follow, and the rest are in the menu. On a record,
the {one_cloud}, print and like buttons sit at the right end, before Cancel.

## The Panes

The pane on the left lists the places of the app you are in. The pane on
the right holds what belongs to the record: who it is assigned to, its
files, its tags, who it is shared with, and its activity. Drag a pane's
edge to make it wider; the button at the right end of the actions shows or
hides it. One remembers your widths.

## The Bottom Bar

The bottom bar says what is here: when a record was made and changed, or
how many rows a list holds and how many it shows at once.
