# Dialogs

Every window that opens over a screen is one of five kinds, and each kind
always looks the same.

## Questions

When {one} needs you to confirm something, the question is the title and
the line under it says what will happen. The button names the act, such
as **Delete Customer**, and is red when it cannot be undone.

## Messages

A message has a round mark before it, red when something went wrong, and
**OK** to close it.

## Tasks

A task, such as sending a statement, has its fields in one column and its
buttons at the bottom right. The line at the bottom left says what is
still needed. The arrows beside the cross make it fill the window.

## Making a Record

Adding a record opens a short form of the fields it needs. Save stays
dark until everything it needs is filled in, and the line beside it says
what is still missing. The link under the fields opens the whole form.

## Centres

AccessCenter, NotificationCenter, {trust_center}, AICenter and {account_center}
open over the screen, with their sections down the left. A page inside a
section has an arrow before its title that goes back. The question mark in
the corner opens Help for the section, the arrows make a centre fill the
window, and it opens that size next time. Close one with the cross or the
Escape key.
