# A Record

## Its Head

A record opens with its name, its state beside it and its number under
it. Where the record moves through steps (ordered, received, billed, paid),
the steps sit under the name, each with its date or what is late. A few
figures may follow, such as what is still owed.

## Its Tabs

The record's fields are grouped in tabs under the head. A tab with nothing
in it is not shown.

## Its Panel

The panel on the right holds Assign, Attachments, Tags and Share, then the
record's Activity, Mail and Connections. Activity shows comments with the
person's picture, and changes as a line of small dots; a group of changes
opens with its arrow.

## Its Shortcuts

Under the panel's first rows are the record's shortcuts: making the next
record from this one, such as a leave application for an employee, or
opening a list of what belongs to it. A shortcut shows only when it
applies, and only to people who may use it.
