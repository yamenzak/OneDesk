A record's buttons now sit in the address row, beside its name and star, so Save and Submit stay in reach as you scroll and the record gains a row; when they do not fit beside a readable address they keep a row of their own under it. A record's tabs and its panel's tabs are the same rounded tabs as a list's, the chosen one tinted. The panel no longer opens on a tab the head has already said. A table inside a record has the same tinted heads as a list, the tabs start on the page's edge, and the details switch shows the tint while the pane is open. A record's sections are now cards of their own on the page, in the same material as the address row and the side pane, and fill the page's width. Its progress sits across from its name. A field to type in is filled like the other controls, with no edge until you type in it, and switches that are off can be seen. Anything that scrolls passes under the address row with a little room, not against it. On a phone or beside an open pane, a record's fields fall to two columns and then one as its page narrows, and its name stands above its progress. A table with no heading of its own stays in the card above it, so an invoice's items are one card with their totals. A required field still empty shows its red edge again. A choice field opens the same list a linked field does, in place of the browser's own, and its arrow sits in the middle of its box. Fields inside a table are cells again, not boxes.
