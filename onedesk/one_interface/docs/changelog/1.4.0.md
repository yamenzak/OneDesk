A record's buttons now sit in the address row, beside its name and star, so Save and Submit stay in reach as you scroll and the record gains a row; when they do not fit beside a readable address they keep a row of their own under it. A record's tabs and its panel's tabs are the same rounded tabs as a list's, the chosen one tinted. The panel no longer opens on a tab the head has already said. A table inside a record has the same tinted heads as a list, the tabs start on the page's edge, and the details switch shows the tint while the pane is open. A record's sections are now cards of their own on the page, in the same material as the address row and the side pane, and fill the page's width. Its progress sits across from its name. A field to type in is filled like the other controls, with no edge until you type in it, and switches that are off can be seen. Anything that scrolls passes under the address row with a little room, not against it. On a phone or beside an open pane, a record's fields fall to two columns and then one as its page narrows, and its name stands above its progress. A table with no heading of its own stays in the card above it, so an invoice's items are one card with their totals. A required field still empty shows its red edge again. A choice field opens the same list a linked field does, in place of the browser's own, and its arrow sits in the middle of its box. Fields inside a table are cells again, not boxes. Every kind of field now looks like the others: each fills its column, a required value is no longer bold, text boxes grow with what is written instead of starting tall and empty, and the rich text, code, signature, attach and many-choice fields are one filled box like the rest. Icons inside fields (a colour's swatch, a password's eye, an attached file) sit on the middle line of their box, and a read-only JSON value is one box whose tools are icons and whose search has no box of its own. A JSON field that can be edited is now edited as the same tree: click a value or a key to change it, add or remove rows from the icons on each row, and switch to the raw text when pasting. Every other way of looking at a list now has the list's own head, with its name, New and the ways to switch view at the far end: a report, a board, a Gantt chart, a calendar, a map, the picture view and a dashboard. A report's table has filled heads and filter boxes, a Gantt chart's bars can be seen, a calendar sits on the same sheet as the rest, and the map view, which opened blank, shows its map. A chart of accounts and a report now carry the same head too, their filters in the row under the address and a report's own buttons at the far end. A chart's legend figures can be read in the dark theme. In the dark theme a menu now stands on the same level as a dialog, a shade apart from the page. A list's rows now sit on a card like a report's, and every table's column headings share one band, the same tone as the filter boxes above them. A report's table has the list's rounded corners, edge and inset. The plain palette is now called Slate, and on it a field in a card shows as a soft grey box like everywhere else.
