A record's buttons now sit in the address row, beside its name and star, so Save and Submit stay in reach as you scroll and the record gains a row; when they do not fit beside a readable address they keep a row of their own under it. A record's tabs and its panel's tabs are the same rounded tabs as a list's, the chosen one tinted. The panel no longer opens on a tab the head has already said. A table inside a record has the same tinted heads as a list, the tabs start on the page's edge, and the details switch shows the tint while the pane is open.
