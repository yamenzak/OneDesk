- On a phone, tapping an app under **Apps** opens it, and inside an app the
  **☰** button before the page's name opens the app's places from the side,
  as most phone apps do. A record keeps its back button there.
- The **☰** menu shows the app's mark and name at the top, and its
  **Settings**, tour, **Help** and version at the foot, as the side panel
  does on a computer.
- On a phone and a tablet, a record's head shows all its buttons, wrapping
  onto a second line: a submitted request's **Create** and **Stop**, and an
  item's **Create** and **Order More**, were hidden or off the screen.
- A record's **Activity** says what you did in a sentence that reads right
  in every language: "Sie haben 2 Felder geändert", not "Sie hat".
- The top bar and the **☰** menu on a phone carry the company you work in,
  when the workspace has more than one.
- A card at the top of a record that says when, such as an opportunity's
  **Next Step**, says when instead of a placeholder.
- Every record's top is marked for tours, so an app can tour a record type.
