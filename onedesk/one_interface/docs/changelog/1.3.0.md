Every centre, Help and new record has the same corner: Help, the arrows that fill the window, and the cross. A page inside a centre has its back arrow before its title. A new record opens as wide as its form needs: with its sections down the side, wide enough for two columns beside them; a short one, small and as tall as its fields. Centres no longer have a margin around their sections.
