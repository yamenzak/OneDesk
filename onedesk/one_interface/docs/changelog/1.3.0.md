Every centre, Help and new record has the same corner: Help, the arrows that fill the window, and the cross. A page inside a centre has its back arrow before its title. A new record opens as wide as its form needs: with its sections down the side, wide enough for two columns beside them; a short one, small and as tall as its fields. Centres no longer have a margin around their sections. A guided tour no longer draws the part it points at over a dialog you opened.

Toggle Theme opens Appearance: the look, Light, Dark or System, and a palette. One now starts in Sand, warm greys with rounder corners; Standard is a click away. Each choice is a small picture of One in it, shows at once, and follows you to any computer. The window now sits the same small distance from the top bar, the bottom bar and the edge, and the row of icons at the foot of the side pane lines up with your picture beside it. The bar with the address and the buttons under it now sit on the same ground as the record: the address row floats as one rounded strip, and the buttons sit on the page beneath it. Everything on a page now starts on one edge, and a list's column names sit in a filled band that stays at the top and keeps its shape when the list scrolls sideways. A list's heading shows its name with the same icon as its row in the sidebar, New beside it and the views at the far end; the count is on the All tab. Its quick filters sit under the address, with Filter and the sort as icons at the end, and the … menu ends the address row. The column names sit in a borderless band washed with New's colour, and the states over a list are tabs of the palette. The side pane is now an island like the address row, the same colour and corners, their tops level. Liking a record is now starring it: the heart is a star everywhere, it fills gold with a small pop, and on a record it sits at the end of the address row, where a browser keeps its own. What is chosen, the row in the side pane, the state tab, the view and the column names, now wears the same soft tint of New's colour; every other control there is the same height, corner and fill, and the empty quick filters say what they are in a readable grey.
