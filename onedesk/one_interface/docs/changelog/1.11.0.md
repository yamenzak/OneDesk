The panel beside a record can show the records it names, such as a project's
customer, each opening its page, and keeps **See on Website** beside them.

A screen with no buttons over it no longer leaves an empty band under the
address row, and on a phone a screen that searches itself shows one search
button, not two.

OneMail's folders now wear the same heading, **New** and states as every list.

On a phone, the company you work in is changed from the top of **Apps**, beside **Documents** and **Check In**, as it is from the bar at the top on a computer. An app's menu holds only that app's places.

Backspace and Delete work again in a screen's own search box, such as OneMail's.

**Back** in the browser returns to the app you came from with that app's own menu, instead of showing its page inside the menu of the app you were in.

OneCloud wears a list's head: the folder's name with **New** beside it and the views at the far end, the files in a card, and what you can do with the chosen files in place of the column headings.
