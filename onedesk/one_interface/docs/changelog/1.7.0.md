On a phone, the bar at the top runs edge to edge like the tabs at the
bottom, and moves out of the way while you scroll down. Its **Search**
searches everything, as Ctrl+K does on a computer, with **Ask OneAI** for
a question. A list's own search is now in the row under it, beside its
filters.
