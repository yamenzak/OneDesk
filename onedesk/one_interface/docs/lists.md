# A List

## Ways of Looking

The buttons at the right of the actions change how the list is drawn: as
rows, a report, a board, a calendar and the other views the records allow.

## Narrowing It Down

**Filter** and **Sort** sit together at the right of the actions. Over
the rows, a strip of states shows how many records are in each, and
clicking one shows only those. The fields beside it narrow the list as you
type.

## The Rows

Bold rows are ones you have not opened yet. At the end of each row are its
comments, who it is assigned to, when it last changed, and its heart.
Drag a column's edge to make it wider.
