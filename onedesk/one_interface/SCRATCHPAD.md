# OneInterface: scratchpad

Known issues, plans and open questions, kept short. Something done or
answered leaves it.

## Planned

- **Looks.** The Canvas look from the UI-language walk, as a look among
  others (`data-one-look` on the page, its own stylesheet, the renderer's
  structure shared): the head with its track and cards, the strip, the
  activity thread, field and tab icons, the outlined panel.
- **One dialog kit** for the seven kinds: Ask, Tell, Toast, Task, Create
  (standard, search first, kind first), Centre, and the islands' dialogs.
- **Island pages** carry a stamp in the bottom bar until each is replaced
  by a framework screen in the screen-by-screen walk.
- A list's column icons are drawn once and not again after the list
  refreshes; the renderer needs to redraw them on refresh.
