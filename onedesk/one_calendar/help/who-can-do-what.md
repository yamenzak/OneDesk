# Who Can Do What

Everybody keeps their own calendar: they see what they may open, make events
and invite people, and keep their own calendar link. Putting an event on
everyone's calendar is for fewer people.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Keep their calendar, make events and invite people, and keep their own calendar link. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also put an event on everyone's calendar, such as a closure or a training day. |

## Without the Role

A Workspace Administrator can do everything, and switches calendar links on
or off for the workspace.
