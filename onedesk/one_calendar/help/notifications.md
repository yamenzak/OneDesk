# What You Are Told

- **Invited to an Event**: someone adds you to an event.
- **Event Changed**: the time or the place of an event you're on changes.
- **Event Cancelled**: an event you're on is cancelled or deleted.
- **Taken Off an Event**: someone takes you off an event.
- **Starting Soon**: at the event's reminder times, or ten minutes before if it has none. An event you said no to doesn't remind you.
- **Today's Events**: each morning, what's on your calendar that day. Not sent on a day with nothing on it.

Whoever made a change isn't told of it. Each comes to the bell, and by email
or push as you choose in {notification_center}, which **Notifications** in
{one_calendar}'s settings opens.

## Guests Outside

A guest outside the workspace is mailed the event to add to their own
calendar, a change as a change, and a cancellation that takes it off their
calendar. Their reply goes to the mailbox of whoever made the event.
