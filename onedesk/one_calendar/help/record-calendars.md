# A Record's Calendar

**Calendar** on a project, a deal, a lead or an employee opens a calendar of
that record alone. It shows every event about the record, including ones you
couldn't otherwise open, which open the record instead, and its own dated
things:

- **A project**: its open tasks, whoever they're assigned to, and its sub-projects' too
- **A deal** or **a lead**: its next step, whoever owns it
- **An employee**: their leave, if you may see it

**New Event** there makes an event about the record, so everybody who may
open the record sees it. The calendars you show or hide there don't change
your own calendar.
