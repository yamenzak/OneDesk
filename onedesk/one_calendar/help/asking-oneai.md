# Asking {one_ai}

On the calendar, the {one_ai} panel offers:

- **What is on this week?**
- **Find a time to meet…**: finish the sentence with who to meet.
- **Plan my day**

{one_ai} reads your calendar as you see it. When finding a time, it sees only
when colleagues are busy, never what their events are. It suggests an event
on a card, and **Approve** puts it on your calendar and invites the people on
it. Nothing is made or sent before that.

An appointment that arrives by mail or in a file {one_ai} reads becomes an
event too.
