# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneCalendar's settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Your Calendar Link** | Your calendar in another app. See [In Google, Apple or Outlook](calendar-link). |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneCalendar's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [What You Are Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who may put events on everyone's calendar. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneCalendar's part of the agreements. |
