OneCalendar opens like every list in One: the days shown head the page, with **New Event** beside them and **Month**, **Week**, **Day** and **List** at the right, now readable on a phone too. Click the days to go to a date. **Today** and the arrows sit at the end of the address row, and the calendar is in a card.
