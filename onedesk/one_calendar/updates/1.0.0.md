OneCalendar is the one calendar for your work in One. This is the version it
is released at.

- Your meetings, your tasks and the dates your records keep, such as a
  contract ending or an invoice falling due, all show on one calendar.
- Turn each calendar on or off on the left, and drag an event to move it.
- Invite people in the workspace, or send an invitation to anyone else by
  email.
- A customer, supplier or project has its own calendar of everything that
  touches it.
- Put your calendar on your phone with a private calendar link.
- AccessCenter decides who may put an event on everyone's calendar, and
  NotificationCenter how each person hears about invitations and changes.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneCalendar Is.
