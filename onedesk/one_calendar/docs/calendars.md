# Your Calendars

Tick a calendar in the rail to show it, and untick it to hide it. Each box
is in its calendar's colour.

## Mine

- **My Events**: events you made, were invited to, or that were shared with you
- **My Tasks**: tasks assigned to you, on their due date
- **Assigned to Me**: anything else assigned to you, such as a deal or a leave application, on its due date
- **Opportunity Next Steps** and **Lead Next Steps**: on your own open deals and leads, at the time due
- **My Leave**
- **My Interviews**: interviews where you're on the panel
- **My Maintenance**: equipment maintenance assigned to you, on its due date
- **Deadlines**: due dates read from bills and letters you can open

## Workspace

- **Company Events**: events on everyone's calendar, and events about a record you can open
- **Holidays**: the company's holiday list
- **Who's Off**: approved leave, off until you tick it
- **Last Day to Cancel**: the last day to give notice on a contract
- **Expiring Documents**: employees' documents that run out, such as an ID or a certificate, off until you tick it
- **Maintenance Due**: all equipment maintenance that's due, off until you tick it

## Only What You May See

Each calendar shows only what you may open. Someone who can't read leave has
no Who's Off, and an employee who sees only their own record sees only their
own leave.

Click anything to open it. A next step opens the deal or lead, a task opens
the task, and an assignment opens its record.
