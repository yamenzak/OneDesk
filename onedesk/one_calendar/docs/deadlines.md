# Lists and Deadlines

**Event** in the rail lists every event you can see, with filters and
search, when each starts, and whether it is open, completed or cancelled.

## The Deadlines List

**Deadlines** in the rail lists every date something must be done by,
soonest first:

- due dates read from bills and letters
- the last day to cancel a contract
- employees' documents that expire, with their number

Each row has the date, the **Days Left**, which turn red within a week, what
the deadline is, and the **Rule** it was counted by. It starts today and
looks 90 days ahead. Change the two dates, or choose **Documents**,
**Contracts** or **Employees** under **Found In**, to narrow it. Click a
deadline to open its record.
