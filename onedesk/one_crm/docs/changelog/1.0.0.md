OneCRM keeps the people and businesses the company sells to in One: who
got in touch, what they might buy, what was said and what happens next.
This is the version it is released at.

- A lead becomes an opportunity, a quotation and an order, each the same
  record the rest of One reads.
- Every lead and opportunity has a next step, told to its owner when it
  comes due and shown on their calendar.
- Home says how selling is going, with the pipeline, what was won and what
  needs somebody today.
- Merging a duplicate lead is now Merge Duplicate Leads, an ability of the
  Sales Manager's that Roles and Access can give to others.
- The agreements on the Get in Touch form and on campaigns now say it one
  point to a sentence.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneCRM Is.
