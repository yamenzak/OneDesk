- **Several companies.** With more than one company, leads, opportunities,
  quotations, orders and support records show their **Company**, starting as
  yours, and every list opens on your company. The **Pipeline**, Home and
  every figure are your company's alone, in its currency.
- An opportunity is won when a sales order is made from its quotation.
  Moving it to **Won** before then says so, instead of failing.
- A customer declining a quotation on the portal works again.
- On **OneCRM**'s Home, **L**, **O** and **Q** start a lead, an opportunity
  and a quotation. Change them in **Shortcuts**.
- An opportunity's **Next Step** card says when it was due.
- An opportunity's page shows its quotation, order and invoices whichever
  company you are working in.
- Help's pages show the screens they describe, and say how winning works.
- Home's six figures stand three across, so none is cut on a tablet or in
  German.
- Appointments, email campaigns, email groups, warranty claims and
  maintenance schedules say what matters at the top and count their lists
  by status: a claim's warranty and when it was resolved, a schedule's next
  visit and how many are made, who an appointment is with.
- An opportunity and a lead have a tour of their own.
- **Past Expected Closing Date** no longer lists an opportunity with no
  closing date.
- A figure with nothing in its months draws no chart.
