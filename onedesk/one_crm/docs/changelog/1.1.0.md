Home's Reports lists only reports you can open, and they open with their
filters. Pipeline cards show amounts in your currency. A lead OneAI makes is
nobody's until somebody takes it. Overview is the sales charts, and the
opportunity form fills its contacts and offers Reopen only once it is closed
or lost. Lead Sources shares out its totals properly, and the forecast counts
a deal overdue once its closing date has passed.
