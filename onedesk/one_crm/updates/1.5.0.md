{one_crm}'s sales assistant now makes what {one_ai} reads for you: a **Lead** from an enquiry, a **Customer** from an order, the people who wrote as **Contacts**, a draft **Sales Order**, a draft quotation for extra work, and a **Contract**. Each is under the sales assistant's name, with **Undo**. A contract waits for you to approve it. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}.

{one_crm}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
