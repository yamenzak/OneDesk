# Being Told

What OneCRM tells people, and when. **Notifications** in Settings
changes how each person hears of each one.

- **Appointment Confirmation**: when somebody books an appointment, and again once it is confirmed, in Appointment Booking Settings' words. To whoever booked it.
- **Contract Expiring**: when a contract with a customer or supplier ends in two weeks. To whoever made it, and the user it names.
- **Email Campaign**: on a campaign's schedule, in the templates it names. To the campaign's leads and contacts.
- **Given to You**: when somebody makes you the owner of a lead or an opportunity. To its new owner.
- **Issue Answer Late**: when an issue has had no first answer by the time its service level agreement gives. To whoever the issue is assigned to, else the support team.
- **New Lead Waiting**: when a lead arrives that nobody owns, from the website or the inbox. To sales.
- **Next Step Due**: when the next step on one of your leads or opportunities comes due. To the lead's or opportunity's owner.
- **Proforma Invoice by Mail**: when somebody sends a proforma invoice from its order. To the addresses it is sent to.
- **Quotation Accepted**: when a customer accepts one of your quotations, on the portal or through a link. To whoever made the quotation, the opportunity's owner and the project's manager.
- **Quotation Declined**: when a customer declines one of your quotations, on the portal or through a link. To whoever made the quotation, the opportunity's owner and the project's manager.
- **Quotation Running Out**: when an open quotation has three days left to hold. To whoever made the quotation, the opportunity's owner and the project's manager.
- **They Replied**: when mail comes in on a lead or an opportunity you own. To its owner.
