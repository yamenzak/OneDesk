# Who Can Do What

Everybody in sales sees every lead and opportunity; a team that wants each
person to see only their own sets User Permissions. A **Sales User** makes
and edits leads, opportunities and prospects, and logs and corrects calls. A
**Sales Manager** also deletes a lead or an opportunity.

**Merge Duplicate Leads** folds a lead that is somebody already known into
the one kept, which deletes it. It starts as a Sales Manager's, and Roles and
Access can give it to others.
