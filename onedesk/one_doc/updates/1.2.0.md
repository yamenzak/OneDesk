OneWriter is called **OneDoc** from now on. Your documents, templates,
versions and comments are where they were and work as before; only the name
on the screen, in Help and in the agreements is new. A record's text opens
with **Open in OneDoc**, and lists call them **Documents**. Its roles are
now **OneDoc User** and **OneDoc Manager**, held by the same people.

{one_doc} has its own place on the rail: **Recent Documents** lists every document you can open, the last changed first, and **New Document** makes one in your own files. An administrator can now turn {one_doc} off. Documents already made still open, read-only, and still download as Word.

{one_doc} has a page in {one_marketplace}, for administrators: what it does, what it brings, whether your plan includes it and the credits it uses.
