OneWriter writes documents, kept in OneCloud beside the rest of your files.
This is the version it is released at.

- **New › Document** in OneCloud makes one, blank or from a template your
  business keeps.
- Headings, lists, tables, pictures and page breaks, with an outline of the
  headings, page setup, and a lock against accidental typing.
- Everybody with a document open writes in the same copy, and comments sit
  in the margin.
- Version History keeps what a document said, and goes back to it.
- Print under your letter head, keep the PDF in OneCloud or on the record,
  and open the document in Word.
- A document on a record prints the record's fields, and a record's text
  opens in OneWriter.
- OneAI rewrites, adds to and makes documents as cards you approve, and makes
  a picture from a sentence. This adds a part to the AI Addendum.
- AccessCenter decides who may keep company templates.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneWriter Is.
