OneWriter is called **OneDoc** from now on. Your documents, templates,
versions and comments are where they were and work as before; only the name
on the screen, in Help and in the agreements is new. A record's text opens
with **Open in OneDoc**, and lists call them **Documents**. Its roles are
now **OneDoc User** and **OneDoc Manager**, held by the same people.
