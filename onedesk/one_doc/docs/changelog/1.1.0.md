When a Word file has things OneWriter doesn't keep, such as comments,
tracked changes or headers and footers, a bar names them and what you write
stays in OneWriter until you choose **Save Over Original** or **Save as
Copy**. Saves within 10 minutes of each other are one version, and while
somebody edits a file in OneOS it opens read-only here and says who.

A document made in OneWriter now has **Edit** in OneCloud: **Edit in
OneWriter**, **Edit in OneOS** to work on it in a full office app, or
**Download as Word**. Each save from OneOS comes back into the document,
with what it was before kept in **Version History** as *Before a OneOS
save*, and anything it couldn't keep is named the next time you open it.
