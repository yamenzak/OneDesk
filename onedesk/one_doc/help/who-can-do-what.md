# Who Can Do What

A document is a file in {one_cloud}, so whoever may open the file may open the
document, and whoever may change the file may change it. Sharing it is
sharing the file. A document on a record follows the record.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Write documents, and work on the ones shared with them. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also keep a document as a template for everyone. |

## What Each Role Starts With

This is what {one_doc}'s roles hold when it arrives in a workspace.
**Roles** in AccessCenter changes any of it, and an update never undoes a
change you made.

{roles}

## Without the Role

A Workspace Administrator can do everything, and changes or deletes any
template.
