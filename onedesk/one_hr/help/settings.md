# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneHR's settings. Somebody with no
lists to keep goes straight to their notifications.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Holidays** | For an administrator: the public holidays and weekly days off leave and attendance count around. See [Setup and Holidays](setup). |
| **Lists** | The values OneHR's records pick from, such as departments, designations and branches. Each opens as its own list. |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneHR's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who may do what in HR. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneHR's part of the agreements. |
