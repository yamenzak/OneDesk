{one_hr}'s people officer now files what {one_ai} reads for you: a **Job Applicant** from a CV, a person's identity documents and certificates on their **Employee**, a sick note as a **Leave Application**, and a colleague's receipt as their **Expense Claim**, left as drafts for you to submit. Each is under the people officer's name, with **Undo**. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}.

When somebody changes an employee's bank details, payroll's bank entry and any payment to them wait until a second person, an HR manager or an administrator, approves the change. The employee is told on their bell.

{one_hr}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
