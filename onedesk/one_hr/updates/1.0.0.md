OneHR is HR for one company in One: people, attendance, leave, pay,
expenses, hiring, growth, and joining and leaving. This is the version it is
released at.

- Everybody checks in with a passkey, asks for leave, claims expenses and
  sees their payslips; HR answers it all from Home's Today.
- Payroll, end of service and expense claims post to the books by
  themselves.
- Hiring runs from the opening to the offer, with recorded interviews when
  the applicant agrees.
- Deleting an interview's sound early is now Delete an Interview's Sound, an
  ability of the HR Manager's that Roles and Access can give to others.
- Everybody can open OneHR's settings now, for its lists, notifications and
  agreements; Holidays stays the administrators'.
- The agreements on check-ins, employees, applicants and OneAI now say it
  one point to a sentence.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneHR Is.
