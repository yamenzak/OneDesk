# Who Can Do What

Everybody who works here sees their own record, payslips, leave and claims,
and asks for leave, expenses and letters. A supervisor also sees the people
who report to them, but not their pay, and what they are asked to approve.
Somebody who manages another app, the stores or the books, is kept to the same
in OneHR. Accounts reads every expense claim and advance, to pay them. HR's
dashboards, the org chart and the
reports about everybody are HR's. An **HR User** keeps everybody's
records, answers requests, marks attendance and runs payroll. An **HR
Manager** also sets the rules and sees a grievance marked sensitive. See
[Attendance](attendance) for who does what with clocking in.

**Delete an Interview's Sound** deletes the sound of a recorded interview
before its retention does, keeping what was written from it. It starts as an
HR Manager's, and Roles and Access can give it to others.
