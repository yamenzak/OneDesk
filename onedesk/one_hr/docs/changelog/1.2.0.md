- **Several companies.** With more than one company, employees, attendance,
  leave, claims, payslips, payroll, hiring and reviews show their **Company**,
  starting as yours, and every list opens on your company. Home is your
  company's, in its currency.
- A leave application, a leave allocation, an attendance, an expense claim,
  an advance, a payslip, a payroll run, an applicant, an interview, an
  appraisal and an end of service say what matters at the top, and their
  lists count them by status.
- A leave application says how many days of its type are left once it is
  taken.
- An applicant's and an interview's rating read out of 5.
- Home's charts count people in whole numbers, and a figure with nothing in
  its months draws no bars.
- Ask OneAI who is in, late or absent today, in your company. On an
  applicant's page it reads their latest interview.
- An employee and a leave application have a short tour, and Help has
  pictures of Home, an employee, leave, attendance, a claim, a payslip, an
  applicant, an appraisal and an onboarding.
- Opening a payroll run, or a leave encashment's rate, needs the right to
  read it.
- Leave, attendance, check-ins, claims, advances and travel requests are
  read as Who Can Do What says: your own, the people who report to you, and
  what you are asked to approve. HR reads everybody's, and accounts reads
  every claim and advance to pay them.
- On Home, **L** starts a leave request, **E** an expense claim and **N** a
  new employee.
- Home's figures keep their whole titles on a narrow screen, and in German
  the time section is **Zeit**.
- An employee's leave reads in your language.
- In Arabic, HR Settings, the shift and leave tools, grievances, hiring and
  advances read in Arabic throughout.
- A clock place's map no longer stops on a small screen.
- An applicant's opening reads by its title.
