Nobody approves their own leave or claim, and Approve leads the request in
place of Submit. A supervisor sees their team's requests and work but not
their pay: payslips, pay assignments and the pay reports show each employee
only their own. Somebody outside HR sees only the grievances they raised. A
flagged check-in says what the clock saw. Shift times read 07:00, HR opens
clock devices, the income tax screens are hidden where there is no income
tax, and Home's reports have the rail's names.
