# Summaries

A customer, a supplier, an opportunity and a project open on a short
**{one_ai} Summary** at the top: what matters now, such as money owed or
overdue, orders that are late, what was raised in mail, and what needs doing
next.

## When It Is Written

- When something happens to the record: an invoice, an order, a delivery or
  a payment submitted for it, a mail about it, a task on the project moved.
  Saving a draft doesn't count.
- At most once a day for each record, however much happens.
- **Summarise Again** at the top writes it now, and **Summarise Now** writes
  the first one.

## Which Records Have One

An administrator turns summaries on or off for each kind of record in
[{ai_center}](settings) under **Summaries**, which also shows how many records
have one. Each summary uses credits.
