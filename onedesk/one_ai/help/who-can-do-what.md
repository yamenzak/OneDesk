# Who Can Do What

Everybody can ask {one_ai}, and it reads only what they could open themselves.
What {one_ai} does for the whole workspace is set by a Workspace Administrator,
who can do everything.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Ask {one_ai}, approve their own cards, and keep their own memories. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Write Knowledge, and see what {one_ai} used, job by job, in AICenter. |

## What Each Role Starts With

This is what {one_ai}'s roles hold when {one_ai} arrives in a workspace. **Roles**
in AccessCenter changes any of it, and an update never undoes a change you
made.

{roles}

## What Stays With Administrators

Turning {one_ai} on or off, its tier, reading what arrives, what
{one_ai} does by itself in each module and which records get a summary are
always a Workspace Administrator's.

> **Your conversations are yours.** Nobody else sees what you asked {one_ai},
> or what it remembers about you.
