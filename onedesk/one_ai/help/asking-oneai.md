# Asking OneAI

The round OneAI button in the corner opens the panel on any page. It opens
on your conversations, with a few suggestions for the page you are on, and
only offers what you could do there yourself.

## Asking

1. Type your question in the box, in any language.
2. Press Enter, or the arrow.
3. The answer comes first. Below it are the records OneAI read: one as a
   card, several as a list. **Show more** shows the rest, and **Open List**
   opens them in their own list.

**Attach a file** adds a file to your question, such as a receipt or a CV. A
card made from it, such as an expense claim, keeps the file.

## What It Knows

Whatever page you open OneAI on, it is told what is on it, so "what am I
looking at" has an answer everywhere. **Knows about** under the box names
it, and × asks without it.

| On | OneAI is told |
| --- | --- |
| A record or a list | Its fields and what it says |
| A report | The filters you set, and it reads the rows you see |
| A dashboard | Each chart and number and what it counts |
| Any other page | What that page shows |

> **It reads as you.** OneAI can't read anything you couldn't open yourself.

## Questions About One

A question about how One works is answered from these guides, with the
section named. If the guides don't cover it, OneAI says so. **Knowledge**,
which administrators write for everyone, such as a policy or a glossary, is
read too.

## Conversations

**New conversation** starts again, and the arrow at the top left lists all
of yours. After its first answer, OneAI names the conversation; click the
name to change it. The panel shows how many credits a conversation used.
