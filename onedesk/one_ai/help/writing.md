# Writing in a Field

Click in a text field, such as a description or a note, then open {one_ai}. It
opens writing that field, named under the box with × to stop.

## What It Offers

| The field | {one_ai} offers |
| --- | --- |
| Empty | **Write a first draft** |
| With text | **Improve it**, **Make it shorter** and **Fix spelling and grammar** |

You can also say what to write in your own words. The text goes into the
form, next to anything you haven't saved, and you save as usual.

## The {one_ai} Mark

While a field still says what {one_ai} wrote, its label carries the {one_ai} mark,
**Written by {one_ai}**. Once you change the text, the mark goes. Any other
field {one_ai} filled, a date, an amount or a customer, shows the same mark
while it still holds what {one_ai} put there.

## Other Fields

Click in any other field, a setting you are not sure of say, then open
{one_ai}, and the question is about that field: it explains it and suggests a
value on a card.

## In a Mail

With a mail window open, opening {one_ai} writes in its message, around your
signature and the mail you are replying to, never over them.
