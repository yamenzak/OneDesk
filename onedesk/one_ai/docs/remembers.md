# What OneAI Remembers

OneAI keeps short facts about you and your work when you ask it to, or when
you tell it something lasting. The answer then shows **Remembered**, with
**Undo**.

## Your Memories

Only you see them. In [AICenter](settings), **Yours** lists them to change
or delete, and **Add Memory** adds one. A memory can be about a record, and
then OneAI reads it when that record comes up.

## Knowledge

**Knowledge** is what OneAI should know about the company, written by an
administrator for everyone: a policy, a price rule, a glossary. OneAI reads
it as facts, never as instructions, and everybody's answers draw on it.

> **Nothing is remembered without a sign.** Every memory OneAI keeps shows
> **Remembered** at the moment it is kept.
