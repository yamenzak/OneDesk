# Cards and Approving

When you ask OneAI to do something, it suggests the change on a card. A card
shows the record and each field OneAI would set, and **See the changes**
lists them.

## Approving

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** | The change is made, as you and with your permissions. |
| **Refuse** | Everything stays as it was. |
| **Approve All** | Every card of the answer is approved, in order. |

If the record changed after the card was made, the card is **Out of date**
and changes nothing. If approving does not go through, OneAI is told why and
suggests it again, corrected.

## Several at Once

Asked for several things that belong together, such as a new customer and a
quotation for them, OneAI suggests them in one go, a card each. A card that
needs another names it, and approving it approves that one first.

## On an Open Form

An approved card puts the change into the form you have open. Each changed
field shows what it was until you save.

## Suggestions

**Suggestions**, in OneAI's rail, lists your cards as **Waiting
for you**, **Done**, **Refused** or **Out of date**. Home counts the cards
waiting for you.
