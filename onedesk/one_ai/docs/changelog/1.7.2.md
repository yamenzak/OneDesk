- In a long conversation, OneAI keeps a short note of what came before, so it still knows what you asked for an hour ago.
- OneAI finds what you asked it to remember, and what your workspace wrote down for it, by what it means as well as by its words.
- **Approve All** does all of the suggestions or none of them: if one cannot be done, the ones before it are undone.
- When OneAI briefly cannot be reached, it tries again on its own before giving up.
