Which app a Frappe module belongs to is read from the manifests' `owns`: the heirs map, OneStudio's forms, OneAI's tool groups and the print formats script no longer keep lists of their own (P1.5 of docs/run/RUN.md).

AICenter finds which apps have a part in it from their ai.py and their manifest, not from a list of its own.

OneCloud's folder is `one_cloud` and its module One Cloud (P2.1 of docs/run/RUN.md); this module's imports and paths into it follow. Nothing changes for people.

OneSlide is OneDeck: its folder is `one_deck` and its module One Deck (P2.2 of docs/run/RUN.md); this module's imports, paths and words naming it follow.
