In **OneCloud**, **New** can now ask OneAI for a picture, music, a short
video or speech from what you describe, saved in the folder you're in. A
picture and speech arrive in seconds; for music and a video you get a
notification when they're ready. Each is billed in credits like OneSlide's
pictures and videos, and shows in **AICenter** by its name.

OneAI credits are now called **OneCredits**.
