OneAI makes video and music for OneDisplay, and a recorded voice costs what
it should.

- It makes a short video for a slide, or a piece of instrumental music for a
  playlist, from a sentence. Each takes a few minutes and is billed when it
  is done.
- A voice was charged as if it had written thousands of words; it is now
  charged for the sound it made, measured on a real recording.
