OneAI can now speak and draw for OneDisplay.

- It reads a ticket's call and a spoken ad aloud in a natural voice, in the
  tone and the voice you choose, and keeps each recording so it is made once.
- It makes a picture, or a whole designed slide in your brand's colour, from
  a sentence.
- It writes a widget of your own from what you describe.

Each uses credits like any other answer, and AICenter turns it off for
OneDisplay as for any app.
