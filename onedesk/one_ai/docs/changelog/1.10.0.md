- If you work in HR, ask OneAI who is in, late or absent today, in English, German or Arabic.
- On an applicant's or an interview's page, OneAI reads the interview to tell you how it went.
- OneAI knows each company's weekend when you ask about leave.
- OneAI can say what tax you owe, which quotations wait for an answer, who
  is on leave on days to come and whose papers expire.
- OneAI finds an employee or a project to put on a paper as you would pick it, even one you may not open.
- On OneQuality's Home, ask what needs fixing: the open problems, the steps overdue and the goals failing.
- Ask OneAI what mail waits on you, and to give a conversation to a colleague, mark it done, snooze it or remind you.
