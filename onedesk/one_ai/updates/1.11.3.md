What OneAI reads from mail and files is called **Reading** everywhere. It is turned on in **AICenter**, and what it read and did is under **Documents**. The error log and screen readers say OneAI and Reading too.

Opening a link by accident no longer starts a conversation, applies a suggestion or answers one: only pressing the button does.
