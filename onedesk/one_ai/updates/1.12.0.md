The foot of the rail, with **Help**, the tour and the version, is on every {one_ai} screen, not only **Documents**. Its settings open from the arrow at the right of the top bar.

Each app now has an agent of its own, a user with that app's roles; {one_mail}'s receptionist may also read the records a mail is linked to, in the mailboxes OneAI reads. Once agents are set up for your workspace, {one_mail}'s receptionist hands each invoice that arrives by mail to {one_book}'s accountant, which drafts the **Purchase Invoice** for you to check and submit. The receptionist says so on the mail, and **Documents** lists the draft under the accountant's name, with **Undo**. The **Privacy Policy** and **Subprocessors** say what the agents keep at Cloudflare.

If you administer the workspace, {ai_center} has a tab for each app's agent: its roles and approver, each of its actions at **Act**, **Draft** or **Ask First** with how many it did and how many were corrected, what it never does, what it learned, what it did with **Undo**, and its credits this month against a **Monthly Limit** you set. **All Agents** shows them together, with the rules every agent follows, a limit for them all, and the switch that turns every agent off. Each app's {one_ai} switches, such as {one_hr}'s screening, moved to its agent's tab.

What OneAI reads from mail and files is called **Reading** everywhere. It is turned on in **AICenter**, and what it read and did is under **Documents**. The error log and screen readers say OneAI and Reading too.

Opening a link by accident no longer starts a conversation, applies a suggestion or answers one: only pressing the button does.

Turning on **Reading** for a mailbox says that scans and photos use credits.

{ai_center} opens in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above its sections.
