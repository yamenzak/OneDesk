# Plan and Credits

**Plan and Credits** shows the workspace's plan, how many of its seats are
used, how much storage and database it takes, its OneAI credits and the
invoices. Seats, storage, database and OneAI credits are a tile each, with
what is used and how much is left, and each has its own button to buy more:
**Add Seats**, **Add Storage**, **Add Database** and **Buy Credits**. **Change
Plan** moves to another plan. Monthly credits are used first, and credits you
buy never expire.

Paying happens on this page: **Buy Credits** and **Payment Method** open
Stripe's own form in a window here, and nothing leaves One. **Billing
Details** opens Stripe's page in a new tab for the billing address, tax ID
and receipts, which Stripe only shows on its own page.

When the workspace can't reach its account, the page says so and shows what
the account last said; changing the plan, buying and the invoices wait until
it can.

The invoices can go into your own books as a bill from One with one press.

## Closing the Workspace

At the end of Plan and Credits, the person the plan is billed to can
download everything in the workspace and close it. The workspace keeps
working for a few days and everybody is told; then sign-in stops, and it is
deleted a few weeks later.

## Your Own Domain

**Domains** lists the addresses the workspace opens at. Add a domain of your
own, point its DNS where it says, and make it the main address once it
works.
