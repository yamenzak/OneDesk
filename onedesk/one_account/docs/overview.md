# What AccountCenter Is

AccountCenter holds your own account, and for administrators, the
workspace's. Open it from your picture at the foot of the rail, then **AccountCenter**,
or from a link in a mail or notification about your account.

## Yours

Everybody has these:

- **Profile**: your name, photo, language and time zone, and if you are an
  employee, the details you keep up to date for HR.
- **Sign-in**: your password, your passkey, and every place you are signed
  in, which you can sign out of.

## The Workspace's

Administrators also have these:

- **People**: everybody in the workspace, what each may use, and inviting
  somebody new.
- **Devices**: the company's computers and phones, and the passkeys on
  people's own.
- **Plan and Credits**: the plan, its seats and storage, OneAI credits and
  the invoices.
- **Domains**: the addresses the workspace opens at.

Read more in [Your Account](your-account), [People and Devices](people-and-devices)
and [Plan and Credits](plan-and-credits).
