# Your Account

## Your Profile

Change your name, photo, language or time zone and press **Save**. If you
are an employee, your profile also shows your employee record: you keep
your addresses, emergency contact and personal details up to date, and HR
keeps your job and bank details. HR hears when you change where you live or
who to call.

If somebody else saved your profile after you opened it, your save stops
and says so, so nothing they wrote is lost. Open it again and make your
change.

**Your Data** at the end of your profile asks for a copy of everything One
keeps about you, or for your account to be deleted.

## How You Sign In

**Sign-in** shows when your password last changed, whether two-factor sign-in
is on for the workspace, and your passkey. A passkey signs you in with your
face, fingerprint or screen lock instead of a password: press **Register
This Device** on the computer or phone you use.

Below that is every place you are signed in. **Sign Out** ends one, and
**Sign Out Other Sessions** ends all but this one. You hear about every
sign-in from a device you have not used before.
