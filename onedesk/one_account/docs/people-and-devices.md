# People and Devices

## Inviting Somebody

Open **People** and press **Invite People**. Give their address and name,
and choose what they may use in each app. Everybody has One, OneCloud,
OneMail, OneTask and OneCalendar; the other apps are given here. They get a
mail with a link to set their password, which lasts a week.

## One Person

Open somebody in People to change what they may use, make them an
administrator, see where they are signed in and their last sign-ins, and
the records they are held to. **Actions** signs them out everywhere, sends
them a password reset, sends a new invitation if they have not joined, or
turns them off. A person turned off can't sign in, and everything they made
stays.

What each role of an app may do is set in AccessCenter.

## The Company's Devices

**Devices** lists every browser somebody has signed in on. Name the
company's computers and phones and say who uses them, or that they are
shared. **Lost** turns off every passkey on a device at once.
