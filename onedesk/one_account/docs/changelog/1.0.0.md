Your account and the workspace's are in one place now, AccountCenter. Open
it from your picture at the foot of the rail.

- Everybody keeps their profile and how they sign in there.
- Administrators also invite people and change what each may use, name the
  company's devices, follow the plan, its credits and invoices, and add a
  domain of the workspace's own.
- A role can be given Plan and Credits in AccessCenter, so the people who
  keep the books can read the invoices without administering the workspace.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What AccountCenter Is.
