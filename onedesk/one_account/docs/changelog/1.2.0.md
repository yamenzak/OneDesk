Administrators choose the day and hour the workspace updates, under **Updates** in **AccountCenter**. **Update Now** moves it straight away when an update is waiting. By default it updates on Saturday at 01:00 in your time zone.

Administrators can also turn on **Auto Top-Up** in **Plan and Credits**. A credit pack is bought on the saved card when the balance runs low, up to a limit each month.
