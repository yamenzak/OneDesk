When a sales order is submitted for something you make and the stores do not hold enough of, {one_factory}'s production planner now drafts a work order for what is short, due by the order's delivery date. You check it and **Submit** it. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}. The AI Addendum now says so.

{one_factory}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.
