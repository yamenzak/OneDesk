**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_factory} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

**Getting Started** in {one_factory}'s Settings opens {setup_center} on {one_factory}'s steps, the account workshop time is costed to and the work order warehouses. **Set Up** opens the **Workshop** tab.

Its **Settings** open in the same frame as every centre, with a **Search** above the sections.

{one_factory} needs {one_inventory}: installing {one_factory} in {one_marketplace} turns {one_inventory} on too.

{one_factory} has a page in {one_marketplace}, for administrators: what it does, what it brings, whether your plan includes it and the credits it uses.
