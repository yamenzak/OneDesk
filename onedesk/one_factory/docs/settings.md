# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneFactory's settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Getting Started** | What is left to set up, while something is: the account workshop time is costed to, and the work order warehouses. |
| **Workshop** | The company's **Default Operating Cost Account**, where time on a job card is costed, and the **Work In Progress** and **Finished Goods** warehouses a new work order takes. Empty ones are filled with what One suggests; **Save** keeps them. |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneFactory's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who may do what in making. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneFactory's part of the agreements. |
