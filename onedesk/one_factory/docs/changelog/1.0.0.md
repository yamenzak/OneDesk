**OneFactory** is a new app in the dock for everything the company makes: the **Shop Floor**, the **Plant Floor**, **Work Order**, **Job Card**, **Production Plan**, **BOM** and **Subcontracting Order**, with their reports and setup on its own rail. They were under Manufacturing in OneInventory. People who make bills of materials and work orders are given OneFactory in **People**. A bill of materials' **Costing** tab and its cost per unit are shown to the workshop's managers, and to anybody else given **See What Making Costs**.
