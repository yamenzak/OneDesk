OneFactory now opens on **Home**: the open work orders, the steps running now, the late work orders, what is out with suppliers and what was made this month. **Today** lists the work orders past the day they were wanted, those due to have started, work late back from suppliers and drafts left.

- If you made a work order, or manage its project, you are told the morning after it was wanted and is not all made.
- The workshop's cost account and its work order warehouses are on the new **Workshop** tab in OneFactory's settings, and **Getting Started** says while either is missing. They were on Inventory Check.
- The Shop Floor no longer lists a work order twice.
- If you make things, **Subcontracting Order** and **Subcontracting Receipt** on the rail now open for you, to read what is out with suppliers.
- A work order, a bill of materials, a job card and a subcontracting order say what matters at the top, with icons on their tabs, and their lists open on what is still to do. A late work order reads **Late** on its row.
- A bill of materials offers **Work Order** to make one from it; a work order lists its job cards and stock entries.
- With more than one company, each of these shows its company, and the Home and the figures are your company's, in its currency.
- On Home, **N** starts a work order and **B** a bill of materials.
- A supplier's contacts find the work sent to them on the portal, under **Work Sent to You**, and OneAI can answer their mail about it.
- Home no longer counts a work order with no expected delivery date as late.
