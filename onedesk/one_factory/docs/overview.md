# What OneFactory Is

OneFactory is where the company makes things: what each product is made
from, the work orders that make it, the steps on the shop floor, and the
work it sends to suppliers. What it uses and what it makes is stock, kept in
OneInventory, so a finished work order is goods in a warehouse there.

## Finding Your Way

**OneFactory** in the dock opens it on **Overview**: the work orders made
and completed this month, the job cards running, and charts of what was
produced, the operations done, downtime and the work orders still pending.
The rail keeps what people use every day:

- **Shop Floor** and **Plant Floor**, for the people making things. See
  [Work Orders and the Floor](work-orders-and-the-floor).
- **Work Order**, **Job Card** and **Production Plan**.
- **BOM**, the bills of materials. See [Bills of Materials](bills-of-materials).
- **Subcontracting Order**. See [Work Sent to Suppliers](work-sent-to-suppliers).
- **More**: the bill of materials builder, production schedules, sales
  forecasts, the production plan timeline, downtime and subcontracting
  receipts.
- **Reports**: what is being made, what it costs and how long it takes.
- **Setup**: workstations, operations, routings, BOM Update Tool,
  subcontracted items and Manufacturing Settings.

## What Is Kept Where

OneInventory keeps items, warehouses and the stock a work order uses and
makes. OneProject counts what a project's work orders use in its Stock Used.
OneBook keeps the supplier's bill for work sent out.
