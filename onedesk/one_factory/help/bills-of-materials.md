# Bills of Materials

A **BOM** (bill of materials) names what one of a product takes. With
**With Operations** it also names the steps (cutting, assembling, glazing),
the workstation each is done at, and its minutes.

- Its page says its **Cost per Unit** in materials and time, its
  **Operations**, and how many **Work Order** use it.
- **Create › Work Order** on a submitted bill starts making it.
- **BOM Creator**, under More, builds a bill with its parts' own bills in
  one tree.
- **BOM Update Tool**, under Setup, swaps one bill for another wherever it
  is used, and updates the costs of every bill.

The **Costing** tab and the cost per unit are shown only to people who may
see what making costs. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what).
