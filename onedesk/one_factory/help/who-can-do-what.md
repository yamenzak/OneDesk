# Who Can Do What

A **Manufacturing User** makes bills of materials, work orders and job
cards, and works the steps on the Shop Floor. A **Manufacturing Manager**
also runs Manufacturing Settings, the plant floors and which supplier makes
what for the company.

**See What Making Costs** is for the managers to begin with: it shows a bill
of materials' **Costing** tab and its cost per unit. **Roles** in
{access_center} gives it to other roles.
