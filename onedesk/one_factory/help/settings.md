# Settings

**{one_factory} Settings**, first under the arrow at the right of the top bar, opens {one_factory}'s settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Getting Started** | While something is left to set up, opens {setup_center} on {one_factory}'s steps: the account workshop time is costed to, and the work order warehouses. |
| **Workshop** | The company's **Default Operating Cost Account**, where time on a job card is costed, and the **Work In Progress** and **Finished Goods** warehouses a new work order takes. Empty ones are filled with what {one} suggests; **Save** keeps them. |
| **Notifications** | {notification_center}, on {one_factory}'s part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | {access_center}, for an administrator: who may do what in making. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | {trust_center}, on {one_factory}'s part of the agreements. |
