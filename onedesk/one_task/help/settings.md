# Settings

**{one_task} Settings**, first under the arrow at the right of the top bar, opens {one_task}'s settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | {notification_center}, on {one_task}'s part of it: what you're told, and how. See [What You Are Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | {access_center}, for an administrator: who sees every task. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | {trust_center}, on {one_task}'s part of the agreements. |
