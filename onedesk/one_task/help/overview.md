# What {one_task} Is

{one_task} is where all your work is kept. Every piece of work is a **task**: a
personal to-do, a project step, a sub-task or a milestone.

A deal's next step, a leave application waiting for you and a meeting aren't
tasks. They belong to the deal, the application and the calendar. Everything
else you have to do is here.

## Finding Your Way

**{one_task}** in the dock opens **My Tasks**. Its rail has:

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **My Tasks** | A board of everything assigned to you, a column for each status. See [My Tasks and Inbox](my-tasks). |
| **Inbox** | The same board for what's assigned to you with no project yet. |
| **Task** | Every task you can see, as a list with its other views. See [Every Task You See](all-tasks). |
| **Reports** | **Delayed Tasks Summary**, for those who work on projects. See [Repeats, Kinds and Delays](repeats-kinds-delays). |
| **Setup** | **Task Type**, the kinds of task, for those with access. |
| **The foot** | **Settings**, the play button that takes the tour, Help, and {one_task}'s version, which opens What's New. |

**Add Task**, at the top of My Tasks, makes a task. Everything updates on its
own: a task assigned to you, or completed elsewhere, appears without
reloading.

Projects, with their boards, calendars and budgets, are in **{one_project}** and
use the same tasks. A project task assigned to you shows on My Tasks like any
other.
