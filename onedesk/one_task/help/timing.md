# Timing a Task

**Start Timer** on a task's page, or the play button on its card on a board,
starts timing it. **Stop Timer** stops it and shows how much time was added
to your timesheet.

- Only one timer runs at a time. Starting another pauses the first.
- The running task's card shows a stop button instead of play.
- A timer stopped within a minute adds nothing.

## Your Timers

**Timers**, in the bar across the top of the page, opens a small window with
your timers, on every page. The one running shows its time today, counting,
with **Pause Timer** and **Stop Timer**. Paused ones show their time today,
with **Resume Timer** and **Stop Timer**. Pausing adds the time so far to your
timesheet and keeps the task in the list. Stopping takes it off the list.

The time goes on **your timesheet for the week**, made if you don't have one,
with the task, its project and your last activity type. Change the activity
there if needed, and submit the timesheet at the end of the week as usual.
The task's **Actual Time** counts it once submitted.

Only people who can make timesheets see the timer.
