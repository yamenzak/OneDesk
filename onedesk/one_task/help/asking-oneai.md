# Asking {one_ai}

On My Tasks and the Task list, {one_ai} offers **What should I do first?** and **Add a task…**. It
reads your tasks as you see them. A task it suggests comes on a card, with a
due date, project, checklist and, if you asked, a colleague to assign it to.
Nothing is added until you approve the card, and the colleague is told then.

On a task's page, {one_ai} offers **What is left on this task?**, **Break this
into steps**, **Change this task…** and **Give this task to…**, and on its
Timesheet, **Who worked on this?** Steps it suggests come on a card, added
after any existing ones when you approve. A change, such as marking the task
done or moving the day it is due, comes on a card too, and is made when you
approve it.

{one_ai} doesn't tick steps or run timers for you: those say what you did, and
only you know that.
