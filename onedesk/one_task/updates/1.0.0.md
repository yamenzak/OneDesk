OneTask is where all your work is kept in One. This is the version it is
released at.

- My Tasks is everything given to you, by when it is due, and Inbox is what
  has no project yet. Add a task from the top and tick it off where it is.
- A task has its own page: its steps, its sub-tasks, the time logged on it,
  its files and what was said about it.
- Time a task with its play button, and the hours go on your timesheet.
- Your tasks show on OneCalendar, and dragging one moves its dates.
- AccessCenter decides who sees every task, and NotificationCenter how each
  person hears of tasks given to them, done or late.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneTask Is.
