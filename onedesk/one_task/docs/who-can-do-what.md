# Who Can Do What

Everybody keeps their own tasks. Seeing every task in the workspace, not only
your own and your projects', is for fewer people.

## Who Sees a Task

- **Your own tasks**, ones you made or are assigned to, are yours to see and
  change. No one else sees them unless you assign or share them.
- **A project task** is also seen by anyone who can see the project.
- **A template task** is seen by everyone who works in projects. It isn't
  assigned to anyone and doesn't show on My Tasks or the calendar.
- **Share** on a task's page lets someone see a task without assigning it to
  them.

You can work on a task someone else assigned to you, but you can't delete it.
Closing your assignment on a task with no project completes the task; on a
project task, it only takes it off your list.

## The Two Roles

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Keep their tasks and work on the ones given to them. |
| **Manager** | People you choose | Also see every task in the workspace. |
