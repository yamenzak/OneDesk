# Settings

The cog at the foot of the rail opens OneTask's settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | NotificationCenter, on OneTask's part of it: what you're told, and how. See [What You Are Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | AccessCenter, for an administrator: who sees every task. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | TrustCenter, on OneTask's part of the agreements. |
