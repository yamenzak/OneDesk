A task opens on its form, like every other record. Above the fields it says
when it is due and how many days late, how far its checklist has got, what it
still waits on and the hours logged against the plan. **Complete Task** is the
first button. Weight and Color are gone from the form, and Issue and Is Template
show only on a task that has them.
