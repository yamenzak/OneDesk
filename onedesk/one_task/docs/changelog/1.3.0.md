- With more than one company, a task shows its **Company** and the task
  list opens on the company you work in.
- A task you may read but not change opens without a message saying you
  have no access.
- On a board, each column is named in your language.
- In German, a task in progress reads **In Bearbeitung** and a low priority
  **Niedrig**.
- A task says at the top when it is due or how late, its checklist, what it
  waits on and the hours logged, with a short tour, and the list's tabs
  leave out template tasks.
- Help has pictures of My Tasks and of a task.
- **Actions › Stop Repeating** on a repeating task stops it, for anyone who
  may change the task.
