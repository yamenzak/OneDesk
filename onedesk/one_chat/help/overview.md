# What {one_chat} Is

{one_chat} is where the team talks: in channels by department or project, in
direct messages between two people, and with the agents, each the virtual
employee of one app. A conversation is read only by the people in it.

## Finding Your Way

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Channels** | Conversations for a team or a project. See [Channels](channels). |
| **Direct Messages** | A conversation between you and one other person. |
| **With Agents** | Your conversations with an agent, the same ones the {one_ai} panel shows. |
| **Agents** | Una and the agent of each app you use. Click one to talk to it. See [Agents](agents). |

A conversation with something new in it is bold, with how many messages you
have not read.

## Writing a Message

Write in the box at the foot and press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
Type @ to mention a person in the conversation or an agent, and the paperclip
adds a file from {one_cloud} or your computer. See [Messages](messages).

## On a Phone

The list of conversations fills the screen; a conversation, and a thread,
open over it, with a back arrow to return.
