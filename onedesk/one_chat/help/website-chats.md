# Website Chats

A visitor to the workspace's website can write to you from a chat button at
the foot of each page. Their chat opens in {one_chat} for the people of the
channel that answers website chats, under **Website Chats**.

## Answering the Website

An administrator turns it on in a channel: **More › Answer Website Chats**.
One channel answers at a time, so turning it on in another turns it off in
the first. The channel's **Description** is the line the visitor reads under
the workspace's name. **More › Stop Answering Website Chats** takes the chat
off the website.

## A Chat

The visitor gives their name, email address and question, and the chat opens
for every member of the channel, who is told. Its head shows their address,
the page they wrote from and the channel it is in.

- What you write in the chat, the visitor reads.
- A reply in a thread is a note for the team. The visitor never reads it,
  nor a card or what an agent looked up.
- **More › Forward** hands the chat to another channel you are in. Its people
  join the chat.
- **More › Close Chat** ends it. The visitor can no longer write in it and can
  start a new one. **Open Again** lets them write again.

A visitor can start five chats an hour from one network address, and write
sixty messages an hour.

## Answering by Itself

The channel's automations run on what visitors write too, in
{automation_center}. **From a Visitor**, **Starts a Website Chat** and
**Website Page** tell their messages apart. Besides what a channel's
automations do, they can:

- **Reply** with words of your own, such as your opening hours, for a chat
  started **After Hours**.
- **Create a Record**: a lead from the visitor's name and address, never two
  for one address, or an issue raised by them. The chat's menu opens it.
- **Forward the Chat** to another channel, such as sales for a chat started on
  the pricing page.
- **Let an Agent Answer**, with {one_ai} on: the agent answers the visitor from
  your **Instruction** and the chat alone, and looks nothing up. It stops once
  somebody of the team writes in the chat.
