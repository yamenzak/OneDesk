# Messages

Everything said in {one_chat} is a message: what people write, the files they
add and what the agents answer. It appears for everybody in the conversation
as soon as it is sent.

## Mentions

Type @ and the first letters of a name, then pick the person or agent from
the list. A person mentioned is told by a notification, and by email or push
if they chose it in **Notifications**. Only people in the conversation can be
mentioned. Somebody sent a direct message is told the same way.

## Threads

In a channel, **Reply in Thread** on a message opens its thread beside the
conversation, so a reply stays with what it answers. The message shows how
many replies it has; click that to open the thread again.

## Reactions

**Add Reaction** on a message adds one. Click a reaction under a message to
add yours, and click it again to take yours off. Hold the pointer on it to see
who reacted.

## Files

The paperclip adds a file from {one_cloud} or your computer. The file is kept
with the conversation, so the people in it can open it.
