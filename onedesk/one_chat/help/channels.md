# Channels

A channel is a conversation for a team, a department or a project, that
several people are in.

## Making a Channel

1. Next to **Channels**, click **+** and choose **New Channel**.
2. Give it a **Title**, and a **Description** if it helps people find it.
3. Turn on **Private** if only the people you add should know it is there.
4. Click **Create Channel**.

You are its first member. **More › Add People** adds others.

## Finding and Joining

**+ › Browse Channels** lists every channel that is not private, with how many
people are in it. **Join** puts you in one; you read it only once you have
joined. A private channel is found only by being added to it.

## Leaving

**More › Leave Channel** takes you out. You no longer read it, and its
messages stay for the people still in it.
