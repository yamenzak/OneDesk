# Channels

A channel is a conversation for a team, a department or a project, that
several people are in.

## Making a Channel

1. Next to **Channels**, click **+** and choose **New Channel**.
2. Give it a **Title**, and a **Description** if it helps people find it.
3. Turn on **Private** if only the people you add should know it is there.
4. Click **Create Channel**.

You are its first member. **More › Add People** adds others.

## Finding and Joining

**+ › Browse Channels** lists every channel that is not private, with how many
people are in it. **Join** puts you in one; you read it only once you have
joined. A private channel is found only by being added to it.

## Leaving

**More › Leave Channel** takes you out. You no longer read it, and its
messages stay for the people still in it.

## Automations

A channel can answer, pass on or file what is posted in it by itself. Its
automations are in {automation_center}, under **When a message is posted**,
one list for each channel you are in, if your role lets you make channels.
Everybody in the channel can read them.

Each runs on every message a person posts in the channel, in the order of the
list. An **If** checks the **Text**, who it is **From**, whether it is **In a
Thread** or **Has File**, and whether it is **After Hours** or **On a
Holiday**. Then it can:

- **Reply**, in the conversation or in the message's thread. {sender_name} in
  the words is the first name of whoever wrote.
- **Post in a Channel**: the message, with who wrote it, in another channel
  you are in.
- **Tell Someone** in the channel, on the bell and by email or push as they
  chose.
- **Create a Record**: a task or an issue from the message, or a lead from a
  visitor's (Website Chats), linked in its thread.
- **Let an Agent Answer**, with {one_ai} on: the agent you choose answers in
  the message's thread from the **Instruction** and the conversation alone. It
  looks nothing up, so it can say only what you told it.

A message posted by an automation carries the automation's name, and never
starts another.
