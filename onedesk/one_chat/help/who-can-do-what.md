# Who Can Do What

Everybody in the workspace uses {one_chat}. A conversation is read only by the
people in it, and a workspace administrator reads none they are not in.

## The Role

| Role | Who has it | What they may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Everyone | Make channels, join those that are not private, send direct messages and talk to the agents of the apps they use. |

{roles}

## In a Conversation

| Who | What they may do |
| --- | --- |
| **A member** | Read it, write in it, react, reply in threads and add people to a channel. |
| **A member who may make channels** | Make and change the channel's automations in {automation_center}. Every member reads them. |
| **Anybody else** | Find a channel that is not private and join it. Nothing in a private channel or a direct message. |
| **The person a card asks** | Approve, change or dismiss it, as can an administrator. |
