# Agents

Each app you use has an agent, the virtual employee of that department:
{one_book}'s accountant, {one_mail}'s receptionist. Una is the one to ask
first when you are not sure who does what. You see the agents of the apps you
can open.

## Talking to an Agent

Click an agent under **Agents** to open your conversation with it, or a new
one. The {one_ai} panel is the same thing: it talks to the agent of the app
you are in, or to Una, and its conversations are listed under **With Agents**.
**Open in {one_chat}** in the panel opens the one you are in here.

In a channel, mention an agent to ask it in front of everybody, as in "@{one_book},
why is this invoice unpaid?". It answers in a thread under your message.

> An agent answers with what you may see yourself, and only one question of
> yours at a time in each conversation.

## Cards

When an agent prepares something for you, such as a draft invoice, or needs
your approval before it acts, a card appears in your conversation with it.
What {one_ai} proposes while reading your mail and files comes from Una.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** | Does what the card says, as you, with your permissions. |
| **Change** | Edits what the card proposes before you approve it. |
| **Dismiss** | Leaves it undone. |
| **Open** | Opens the draft the agent made, for you to check and submit. |
| **Keep** | Keeps something done that the auditor doubts. |
| **Undo** | Takes back something done that the auditor doubts. |

Only the person the card asks, or an administrator, can answer it.
