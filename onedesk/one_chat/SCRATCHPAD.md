# OneChat — scratchpad

What is known to be missing or wrong. How it works and why is `AGENTS.md`.

- An agent answers through OneAI's one chat loop, with all of OneAI's tools,
  as the person who asked; its own tools, procedures and memory come with
  P8.3. Una does not hand a job to another agent yet.
- An agent's daily note in its department's channel, rooms with the outside
  world, saved replies and automations are P8.4 (docs/ARC-AI.md).
- A message can't be edited or deleted, and a conversation can't be renamed
  from the screen but in the panel.
- Faces are DiceBear's bottts drawn still; the animated face and OneChat's
  mark wait for P10.
- The panel's Change on a card opens OneChat, where Change edits the values;
  a card's table rows are not editable, only its plain fields.
- Read state is per conversation; a thread's replies are not counted unread.
- No Help pictures yet.
