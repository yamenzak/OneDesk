An agent you ask now answers with its own app's tools and nothing else, as far as both of you may go: {one_book}'s accountant answers about the money, {one_crm}'s sales assistant about sales. A record it makes or changes for you is a card in the conversation, to **Approve** or **Change**. Una answers what no app's agent does and asks the right agent for the rest.

Everything {one_ai} read for you that waits for your decision is now a card in your conversation with the agent that did it, or with Una when {one_ai} did it while reading. Something already done that the auditor doubts is a card too: **Keep** it or **Undo** it.

{one_chat}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.

With {setup_center} open, {one_ai}'s panel talks to its setup guide, and a card that fills in a step shows the step's own labels.

A channel can do things by itself now. In {automation_center}, each channel you are in has its own list of automations, if your role lets you make channels. One starts **When a message is posted**, can check the words, who wrote it, whether it is in a thread or has a file, and whether it is after hours or a holiday, and then can **Reply**, **Post in a Channel**, **Tell Someone** or **Create a Record**, a task or an issue linked in the message's thread. With {one_ai} on, **Let an Agent Answer** has an agent of your choice answer in the thread from what you tell it and the conversation alone. A message an automation posts carries its name.
