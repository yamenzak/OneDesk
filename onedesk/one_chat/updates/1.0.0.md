{one_chat} is where the team talks, and where you talk to the agents.

- **Channels** for a team or a project, private or open to anybody to join,
  and **Direct Messages** between two people.
- Type @ to mention somebody, who is told by a notification. Reply in a
  thread, react to a message and add a file from {one_cloud}.
- **Agents** lists Una and the agent of each app you use. Ask one in its own
  conversation, or mention it in a channel and it answers in a thread.
- When an agent drafts something or asks before it acts, a card appears in
  your conversation with it, with **Approve** and **Change**.
- The {one_ai} panel is now a conversation with the agent of the app you are
  in, or with Una, and opens in {one_chat} too.

Its messages are kept in your workspace and read only by the people in each
conversation. This adds a part to the Privacy Policy and the AI Addendum.
