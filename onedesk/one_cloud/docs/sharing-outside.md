# Sharing Outside the Team

A link gives a file or a folder to people outside the team, such as a
customer, a supplier or an accountant, without an account.

## Creating a Link

In the Share dialog, **Create link** makes the link and copies it, so you
can paste it into an email or a chat. You choose:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | **Anyone With Link**, or **Invited People Only**. Each invited person is emailed the link. When they open it, they enter their email address and get a code, so a forwarded link doesn't work for anyone else. |
| **What they can do** | View, download, and for a folder, upload. With upload on, people can send you files without an account, and you're notified when files arrive. |
| **Expires on** | The day the link stops working. |
| **Password** | Asked before a link anyone can open shows anything. |

## What People See

People with the link see the file, or the folder and what is in it now.
Files you add later appear, and files you delete or move out don't.

## Managing Links

A file's Share dialog lists its links and how often each was opened. The
cross removes a link at once.

> Anyone who can edit a file can create a link to it, and anyone who can
> edit a record can create a link to its files.
