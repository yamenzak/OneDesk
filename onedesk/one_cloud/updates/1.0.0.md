OneCloud is where your company's files live, inside One. This is the version
it is released at.

- Your own files, the company's folders, team libraries and every file
  attached to a record are in one explorer, with search across all of them.
- Share a file or folder with the team, or with a customer or supplier
  through a link, and ask people outside for the files you need.
- Open Word, Excel and PowerPoint files in One, or in a full office app in
  OneOS, with every save kept as a version.
- Documents open in **OneDoc** and workbooks in **OneSheet**, the new names
  of the editors that were OneWriter and OneWorkbook.
- Connect a folder as a drive on your computer, or a server as a folder.
- **Settings**, the tour and Help are now at the foot of OneCloud's rail.

The Privacy Policy and the AI Addendum now say what OneCloud does with your
files in shorter sentences. Nothing they say has changed.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneCloud Is.
