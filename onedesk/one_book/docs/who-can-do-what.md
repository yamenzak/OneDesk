# Who Can Do What

Everything in OneBook is for whoever People gives it. An **Accounts User**
enters invoices, bills and payments, matches the bank and imports a
statement. An **Accounts Manager** also enters the opening invoices, adds
banks and suppliers, keeps the tax templates, and does the two things below.

- **Lock the Books**: closing a period or a year, so nothing dated in it can
  change. See [Closing the Books](closing).
- **Fix What the Check Finds**: the changes **Books Check** offers, such as
  adding next year or a bank account. Without it the check still says what
  is missing, with no **Fix**. See [Getting Ready](getting-ready).
