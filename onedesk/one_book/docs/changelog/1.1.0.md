Accounts Receivable opens on payment terms, so it agrees with Home's
Overdue, and Accounts Payable opens on suppliers only. Due This Week opens
what is due this week, and Overdue and Due This Week draw their own small
charts. Reports with nothing to show here are hidden until they can show
something, and an Accounts User now opens Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and
Cash Flow. Bills claim their VAT back on the VAT return, and Books Check finds
one that doesn't. Credit and Debit Notes no longer leave their filter on the
invoice lists, and the Bank Reconciliation Tool opens on what is still to
match.
