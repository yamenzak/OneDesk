{one_book}'s accountant now drafts what {one_ai} reads for you: a supplier's bill as a draft **Purchase Invoice**, a customer's payment advice as a draft **Payment Entry**, a bank statement's lines as **Bank Transactions**. When a reminder names an invoice nobody has, a task to ask for it waits for you. Each is under the accountant's name, with **Undo**, and you submit the drafts. How far it goes is set on its tab in {ai_center}.

A bill from a supplier you have no record of now waits for you to approve the new supplier, and the accountant then drafts it.

{one_book}'s page in {one_marketplace} shows it at work, in pictures and short clips of the demo company.

In a group of companies, booking a bill in another currency to a company in the group no longer stops: its payable in that currency is added across the group.
