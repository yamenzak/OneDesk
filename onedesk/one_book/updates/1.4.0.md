**Settings** moved from the foot of the rail to the top bar: the arrow at its right opens **{one_book} Settings** first, then every other centre you may open and {one_hub}. The foot keeps **Help**, the tour and the version.

If you administer the workspace, each thing {one_book}'s agent may do is named in {ai_center}, in your language.

Once {one_book} has a ledger entry or a submitted invoice, it can't be turned off in **Turn Apps On or Off**, and its card says why.
