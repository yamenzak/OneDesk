OneBook keeps the money in One: the invoices you send, the bills you get,
the payments, the bank and the reports. This is the version it is released
at.

- Home says where the money stands, with what needs doing today.
- Invoices and bills are followed until they are paid, and late payers are
  reminded.
- The bank is matched line by line, and the tax return and closing a year
  are a few clicks.
- Locking the books and fixing what Books Check finds are now abilities, the
  Accounts Manager's to start with, and Roles and Access can give them to
  others.

HelpCenter explains all of it, starting with What OneBook Is.
