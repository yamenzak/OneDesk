# Being Told

What {one_book} tells people, and when. **Notifications** in Settings
changes how each person hears of each one.

- **Bank Details Changed**: when the bank details a supplier or customer is paid into are changed. To the supplier or customer, at the address on their record.
- **Bank Details Waiting for Approval**: when somebody changes the bank details a supplier, a customer or an employee is paid into. To accounts managers, HR managers for an employee, and administrators, but whoever changed them.
- **Bills Due This Week**: on Monday morning, when supplier bills fall due in the coming week. To accounts.
- **Credit Limit Crossed**: when somebody flags a customer who is over their credit limit. To accounts.
- **Deferred Posting Failed**: when the nightly posting of deferred income or expense fails. To the site's operators, so nobody in the workspace.
- **Invoice Overdue**: on the morning after one of your invoices goes past its due date unpaid. To whoever made the invoice and the customer's account manager.
- **New Fiscal Year**: when next year's fiscal year is made on its own, so somebody checks it. To accounts.
- **Paid Online**: when a customer pays an invoice, or an order's deposit, online through its payment link. To whoever made the invoice or order and the customer's account manager.
- **Payment Reminder**: when an invoice is still unpaid a week after it was due, with the invoice attached. To the customer's contact on the invoice.
- **Payment Request**: when a payment request is submitted, with its link to pay, unless it is set to stay quiet. To the customer's contact on it.
- **Payment Waiting for Approval**: when a payment out of the ordinary is saved, and waits for a second person. To accounts managers and administrators, but whoever made it.
- **Repeated by Mail**: when a repeated invoice or bill is made and its repeat is set to mail it, or when a repeat fails. To the addresses on the repeat, and its maker when it fails.
- **Statement of Accounts**: on each statement run's schedule, in the words set on the run. To each customer on the run.
