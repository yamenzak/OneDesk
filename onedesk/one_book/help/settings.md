# Settings

**{one_book} Settings**, first under the arrow at the right of the top bar, opens {one_book}'s settings.

| You see | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Lists** | The values {one_book}'s records pick from, such as tax categories and finance books. Each opens as its own list. |
| **Notifications** | {notification_center}, on {one_book}'s part of it: what you're told, and how. See [Being Told](notifications). |
| **Roles and Access** | {access_center}, for an administrator: who may do what in the books. See [Who Can Do What](who-can-do-what). |
| **Your Data** | {trust_center}, on {one_book}'s part of the agreements. |
